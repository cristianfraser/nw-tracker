# Valuation series, card metrics and returns (nw-tracker)

> `.claude/rules/`. A section named in «…» that is not in this file is in `AGENTS.md` or in the file

## Daily series — calendar-day grid deltas (`dailySeries.ts`)

- **`getBucketDailySeries(accounts, { unit, days, now?, includeAccounts? })`** (`server/src/dailySeries.ts`): one point per **Chilean calendar day** ending today (`chileWallClockAt(now).ymd`; `days: 0` = since `portfolioStartYmd`; `DAILY_SERIES_MAX_DAYS` 7400 is a sanity bound, not a product cap — full-history daily is a user choice). Values are true levels via `bucketValueInUnitAt` (→ `nodeValue`: Σ each member's own value in the unit, `accountValueAt`) — each account's mark is flat on its own closed days (weekend/holiday), so **per-account market-calendar attribution falls out of level deltas** (a `.SN` line moves on Chilean days, USD stocks on NYSE days, crypto/UF every day) with no session-window bookkeeping. Each point carries **`market_day`** (`isNyseTradingDay || isChileBusinessDay`; false = weekend/shared holiday — the detalle table dims those rows). Flows use the same deposit-event accounting as `netDepositFlowBetween`, bucketed per `(prev_day, day]` (bulk `loadMergedDisplayDepositInflowEvents`, USD cash included). `pl = delta − flow`; missing legs → null, never a fake 0. Grid helper: `chileCalendarDaysListEndingAt` (in-file).
- **Money without a value gives no return (`unvaluedMemberDays`).** A bucket sums only the members that have a mark that day, so a member whose money arrived before its first valuation would read as money in without its value (huge ± swings that collapse a daily-chained TOTAL). A run of unmarked days that money entered and that ends in a mark — and the day the mark appears — has `pl` and `pct` null (flat in a chain). Never-valued (not yet open) and no-longer-valued (closed) members are not flagged.
- **Liquidation-edge conventions:** (1) **USD-cash deposits are event-based, native-frame** — `loadForeignCashCapitalSortFlows` (`equityBrokerageCapitalFlows.ts`) emits every balance-affecting non-interest movement as a deposit event (USD sign = `signedNativeDeltaForAccountMovement`, so Σ event USD ≡ balance − interest by construction); the CLP frame is per event, never a re-priced aggregate: compra counter legs at their real pesos, equity legs (`stock_buy`/`stock_sell`/`dividend_payout`) mirror the stock side's attribution exactly (intra-bucket transfers net to the peso), legless USD at the event date's sell rate. **A wire shared by same-day buys is split pro rata (`wireProRataFlows`):** with exactly one CLP→USD conversion on the cash account that day, no buy equal to it, and the day's buys needing at least its dollars, each buy carries `wire pesos × buy / Σbuys` at face plus the dollars the wire did not cover (USD already held, e.g. a swept dividend) at the reference rate — the last buy takes the rounding, so the pieces add up to the wire; one buy larger than the wire is the wire + residual composite. (Without the split, the buys fall to the reference rate while the wire comes in at its real pesos, and the conversion spread reads as a −100% month on the cash account instead of sitting in the stocks' cost.) There is no balance − interest re-pricing anywhere (flows deposits windows, valuation dep maps, daily telescoping, `foreignCashMonthlyPerfRows`): a no-event window reads 0 deposits in both frames; fx moves on held USD land in P/L like equities. CLP ledger cash and checking accounts follow the same rule: the group consolidation sums their merged deposit events per month through `ledgerCashNetFlowBetween` (events load once per account via `netDepositFlowWindows`), and `valuationTimeseries` has no CLP-cash balance − interest branch, so the aportes line, both performance tables, the card and the daily series read the same events. In pesos the events sum to balance − interest on every day; fx moves on pesos held are USD P/L. These monthly rows (USD cash included) give the first month P/L = close − flow, as the account performance builder does. (2) **`pct_month` is sign-guarded** (`flowAdjustedPct(nominal, prior, netFlow, close, unit)` in `perfKernel.ts`, re-exported by `periodReturns.ts` — accountPerformance's closed months, live current-month row and first months, both consolidations, the dashboard pcts (closing at the row's current value), the daily series `pct`, the 1D/1W cells and the equity position's return on deposited all use it). **A period that ends at zero** (`|close| ≤ eps`) on a net withdrawal divides by `prior` (the start frame's `prior + netFlow` is then exactly `−nominal`, which would read every emptying sale as −100%); withdrawing exactly the prior close reads 0%, ending at zero with no prior close (bought and sold inside the period) reads null, and ending at zero on a net deposit keeps −100% (everything at work was lost, never below −100%). **Zero is the unit's coin** (`zeroCloseEps(unit)`: the currency's smallest coin — 1 peso, 0,01 USD —, 0,0001 UF; `flowAdjustedPct` takes the unit) on every grain: a close, prior or start base at or below it is no capital, so a one-peso dust balance reads null instead of dividing the day's fx move by a peso. **Group periods carry each member's zero close** (`endChargedFlow` / `groupStartFrameFlow`): a member that ends at zero on a net withdrawal has it charged at the period end, capped at the group's own net withdrawal so a transfer between members is not counted twice — in `consolidateGroupMonthlyPerf` (rows carry `end_charged_flow`, summed by the yearly rollup), the net-worth / inversiones bucket sums (re-capped), the daily series and the 1D/1W cells; a period where no member empties is unchanged: when `prior + netFlow ≤ eps` (a liquidation withdraws intra-month gains too) the denominator falls back to `prior` — otherwise a close-at-0 month reads exactly −100% by the identity `nominal = −(prior + netFlow)` and poisons every chained Rentabilidad window. (3) **Daily chart lines clip as units** (`trimDailyLineEdges`): a never-held-in-window line nulls its `deposits_acum` too, aggregated bucket lines re-trim after summing, and the daily payload carries **`chart_end_ymd`** (mirror of the monthly block's) when every account line ended — the client trims valuation-chart points only (`buildDailyValuationBlock`); `points` stay full-length for P/L bars and the detalle table. (4) **Grouped bucket aportes aggregate from PRE-trim deposits:** `groupDailySeriesAccounts` sums each bucket's `deposits_acum` from the series' internal `deposit_cums_raw` (every member's raw cums — wound-down and never-held-in-window included — then the aggregate edge-trims as a unit), never from the display-trimmed member lines: summing trimmed lines makes an active bucket with a liquidated member lose that member's terminal net deposits at its clip day, jumping the aportes line by the dead member's withdrawals. A bucket line's terminal aportes equal that bucket's own leaf-page `deposits_acum_total` by construction; `deposit_cums_raw` is stripped from HTTP payloads at the `/api/daily-series` route.
- **One conversion for deposit and flow events (`flowEventInUnit`, `rates.ts`).** Every merged-deposit reader (dashboard totals, `netDepositFlowBetween`, the flows deposits page, monthly/daily aportes, the daily grid incl. mortgage and derived card flows, the fx scan) and the depto ledger payments (mortgage card, performance table, daily view — the property's payments too) convert each event at its own date's rate: a carried leg as is (CLP `amt`, a recorded `amt_usd`), CLP→USD at the buy rate on/before the date (`convertFlow`, unrounded), CLP→UF at that day's UF. Unrounded; a missing rate throws, never a skipped event or a null total, and the flows deposits payload has no `fx_conversion_error`. Never convert a month's sum at the month-end rate (it disagrees with the card and the daily view). Test fixtures a USD reader converts must sit inside the test person's market window (about two years back). Summary «Depositado», chart aportes and dashboard deposits all read personal capital (`movementCountsAsPersonalDeposit`: APV-A's state bonus is not a deposit). Not yet on the rule: the deposits reconciliation, and UF window flows (converted at the window end, the ledger-cash group tables included). A CLP event converts at the buy rate while balances use the mid, so on a cash account a deposit reads a small +USD P/L in its month and a withdrawal a small −P/L; they cancel over a round trip.
- **Group consolidations get each account's payload whole.** Both paths into `consolidateGroupMonthlyPerf` (the cached group consolidation, which also feeds net worth, inversiones and flows P/L, and the group tables' `getGroupConsolidatedMonthlyPerfForRows`, both over `buildConsolidationPayloads`) pass the account payload whole — don't rebuild it field by field: dropping `import_key` makes a Fintual certificate account's month-end mark fall back to its perf rows (already in the requested unit) and the USD path divides them by fx again. `monthEndCloseForAccountInUnit` converts only the mark (`monthEndMarkClpForAccount`) and takes the perf-row fallback as it is, already in the rows' unit; it converts to UF too, and the live current-month row reads UF (close at today's UF, the month's flow at today's UF).
- **Cached wrapper** `getBucketDailySeriesCached(scopeKey, …)` (real clock only) → `daily.series|<scope>|<unit>|<days>|<ids>` keys (cache family `daily_series`), dropped by the change log — any applied write to a table a valuation reads (`cacheRegistry.ts`, `cacheFamilies.ts`) — and by `invalidateMarketDataAggregations`.
- **Book-value carry for manual-marked accounts** (`storedMarkFlowCarry.ts`): the terminal stored-`valuations` forward-fill (historical branch in `accountMarkClpAtYmd` + both generic stored returns in the `accountTodayValue` stack) returns stale mark **+ net personal flows since its date** (`netDepositFlowBetween`, the same reference as the daily/d1 flow legs) — so a deposit day steps the value with `pl = 0` by construction, and the next mark day carries the clean inter-mark P/L. A mark dated the flow day has an empty carry window (no double count). **Never applied to CC or mortgage balances** (kind-gated: CC owed-on-date has its own derivation; a mortgage payment's balance effect is amortization only). This makes the Rentabilidad d1/w1 cells and today's dashboard values deposit-aware for these accounts. In the today stack, depto property/mortgage UF marks resolve **before** the stored return (first in `accountTodayValue`), so a stale property valuation cannot shadow the live ledger mark.
- **Known limits:** monthly-marked accounts (DAP, cuenta ahorro) render P/L as steps — the mark day carries the accumulated inter-mark P/L (carry only moves flows, never fabricates intraperiod returns). A full-history daily build is the expensive one. The depto property/mortgage ledger is memoized (`depto.ledger|rows|<property id>`, family `depto_ledger`, dropped by the change log); the remaining hot spot is CC `latestCreditCardValuationRowAsOf`.
- **The account page header shows the dashboard card's value (`accountTodayValue.ts`).** Both read `accountTodayValue` — the value `accountMarkClpAtYmd` returns at Chile today, every dashboard row, liabilities included, so today shares a frame with the prior-day and month-end marks — which carries one override: accounts valued from units × price (cuota ledgers, Fintual cert funds, crypto) show their position's mark, the position asked at TODAY. Don't add a second value ladder for the header.
- **Per-account mark cache + live-tail re-pricing:** marks dominate a daily build, so each account's mark series is cached on the calendar-day grid (`server/src/accountMarkDailyCache.ts`, `accountMarkClpSeriesOnGrid`; the cells are quantities, see «The daily cache stores quantities») and the daily consumers — `getBucketDailySeries`, `buildDashboardBucketDailySeriesClp`, the overview payload's liabilities leg — read it instead of calling `accountMarkClpAtYmd` per account per day. **Only days strictly before Chile today are cached**; today always recomputes (live quotes, intraday fx). That split is what makes the live-quote poll cheap: no historical mark reads `live_market_quotes` (`accountMarkClpAtYmd`'s live stack is gated on `asOfYmd === today`, and `computeEquityMtmClp` / `computeCryptoMtmClp` convert at `asOfYmd` through `rates.ts` whatever the price source — a historical date never matches the live row's session, so it reads the stored rate), so a live tick drops the daily *aggregations* and keeps the marks. The series **extends** in both directions as wider windows are requested (a later wider view pays only the missing prefix), in their own store (`markSeriesStore.ts`); grids must be contiguous ascending calendar days (checked O(1), throws) and a length/range mismatch throws rather than silently shifting values by days.
- **Mark-cache waterfall (migration 211, `markInputTriggers.ts` + `cacheRegistry.ts`).** The per-account mark series live in their own store (`markSeriesStore.ts`), not the aggregation cache, so they survive the Chile day rollover (a past day's mark does not depend on the date it is read) and every write that does not touch an account's inputs. Each table a historical mark reads has a trigger spec (movements, valuations, the card statement / line / installment / traspaso tables, mirror merges, loan payments, fx (+ bid/ask, observado), UF, fund units, equity closes, the proxy basket, accounts, asset/portfolio groups, market symbols) logging `(account or NULL = every account, raw date)`; the registry trims each affected series to the days before the change by the spec's rule (`backDays` / `recentDays` / `rateTable`: a card's dollar lines read the fx of their pay-by, so an fx row reaches 75 days back for cards; a statement header its billing period; the proxy's observado 7 days; a fund cuota 10 days; the proxy basket 30 days) and drops the daily aggregations and input memos built on them. An equity close logs its ticker's holders only (every account when the ticker is in the proxy basket; nothing for a watchlist-only ticker). The depto property and mortgage accounts are trimmed together (`declareAccountsTrimmedTogether`). A movement that is its account's first units row logs the whole history (it switches the valuation method). `clearAggregationCache()` drops everything, marks included. A source with no spec throws.
- **A live-quote tick rebuilds only today (the history and live tiers in `cacheRegistry.ts`).** A tick drops the page bundle, the monthly-perf / consolidation / flows payloads and the daily aggregations, but what they are built from survives it: the **history tier** (`historyTierMemo`, the `hist.` family) memoizes every input a live quote cannot move — a past day's `accountMarkClpAtYmd` (the month-end marks every monthly builder walks; the daily mark store keeps its own days and bypasses it), the merged deposit timelines (`buildMergedDepositMap`), the ledger month closes before today (`balanceOnlyMonthlyRowsAsc`), equity / crypto MTM at a past day (`computeEquityMtmClp` / `computeCryptoMtmClp` without a live price or `now`), the linked-card netting by past day, a past day's rate per pair and side (`value_rate|…` in `valueConvert.ts`) and a past day's price (`holding_price|…` in `holdingPrices.ts`). It is dropped wholesale by every other invalidation — a write row of any logged table, `historical` market data, a mark row of any connection, the day rollover and `data_version` — so outside a tick it is as fresh as the rest of the cache; a new entry may read only mark-input tables or rows one of those funnels covers, never a live quote, the live fx row or the clock beyond the Chile day. Inside a transaction both tiers hand out throwaway maps (report-only imports read rows they roll back). The **live tier** (`liveTierCached`) memoizes `accountTodayValue` on the real clock for the rest of the UTC minute and is dropped by every invalidation, a tick included. The wall clocks (`chileWallClockAt`, `nyseWallClock`) are memoized per UTC minute (minute resolution, whole-hour offsets, so exact). A tick also drops the cached card billing-detail bundles (`cc.billing_detail|<id>`, not their satellites), because an unpaid facturación prices its foreign amounts at the live rate (`rate_source: live`). Parity: `liveTickIncrementalRebuild.test.ts` (synthetic DB, frozen trading afternoon; rebuild after simulated ticks must equal a rebuild from an empty cache; covers the flows overview too). **The flows overview's inputs follow the live dollar rate, not the tick (`syncFlowsInputsWithLiveUsdRates` in `flowsMortgageCarrying.ts`):** the expense lines price an unpaid facturación's dollars at that same live rate, so the inputs (`flows.overview.inputs|`) record the live rates they used (the cards' facturaciones with `rate_source: live`) and are dropped when a reader finds the cards' current ones differ — a tick that moves only stocks or coins, or one after the fx day closed, keeps them. (The receipt matcher's day arithmetic is memoized and windowed by day, the display dedupe buckets purchases by account/facturación/pesos/day, and the nota-cancelled plan ids sit in the history tier.) Not done: trimming the history tier by date on `historical` invalidations (the mark-series waterfall's rule) instead of dropping it.
- **A dropped payload rebuilds only today's part (`docs/performance-plan.md` «A-F as built»).** On top of the tiers above, the expensive builders keep their past in the history tier: the daily series' past part (`daily_series_past` in `dailySeries.ts`: each account's value in the unit on every grid day but the last, the flow legs, deposit cumulatives and market-day flags), the ledger accounts' closed-month rows (`ledger_closed_month_rows`), a plain account's raw valuation-chart mark on a past date (`valuation_chart_past_value`; never the trailing or today point, never the card / mortgage / property lines), a card's statement list (`cc_statements_by_account`, frozen rows) and the deposit-event buy rate (`flow_buy_rate|<base>|<quote>` in `rates.ts`, rates found only). A card's billing detail is two cached parts: the base (`cc.billing_detail|<id>|base`: ledger payload, detalle, facturaciones without their foreign rates), which a scoped tick drops only when it read a moved price, and the bundle, which copies the facturaciones and applies the rates (`withFacturacionForeignRates`), so a dollar tick re-applies only the rates. Each Pasivos reference source's totals are a cached group-response part (`reference_source_totals`). **`buildHistoryTierValue(what, build)`** (`cacheRegistry.ts`) runs a history-tier part in its own dependency node and throws if it read any live input (a quote, the live dollar, or a gate deciding whether to read one) — use it for new parts; the valuation-chart memo is the one exception, because the equity MTM consults the live-dollar gate even for a past day (it reads the stored close there; `equity_mtm_clp` relies on the same rule). Parity: `liveTickIncrementalRebuild.test.ts` runs a coin-only tick on a Bitcoin account it adds, besides the dollar ticks.
- **Group/account page per-account lines: plain stored-valuation accounts use the mark:** plain stored-valuation accounts — manual marks, DAP, cuenta ahorro, the pre-Fintual «Principal» predecessor accounts — value each chart date through `accountMarkClpAtYmd` (stored + net flows since; null before the first mark), matching the daily series; don't forward-fill the last stored `valuations` row without the flow carry (`buildPointsForAccounts`), or a deposit or liquidation after the last mark leaves the line stale. **Guarded to plain-stored only**: equity/crypto/cash MTM, credit_card, mortgage, afp, property, and Fintual cert keep the raw their own branches compute (those framings are intentional — AFP ledger×px transit months, the Fintual cert freshness gate). **Fintual cert accounts read the live mark** (cuotas × fund-unit price, the certificado value) at every date; they have no stored `valuations` rows, and `augmentChartDatesForFintualCertAccounts` derives their chart date grid from the cuota history (month-ends from the first cuota event, which must include **transfer legs**: aportes are mostly modeled as checking→fund transfers, so a single-leg-only MIN would miss the start). **Edge trims on per-account lines:** both grains null each line's **leading** run of 0/null so a late-starting account begins at its first holding (equity reads null pre-holding; crypto's zero-unit mark is a finite 0 — the trim makes them agree), and the daily mirrors the monthly's **trailing** clip (`trailingZeroRunClipStartIndex`, shared out of `timeseriesTailClip.ts`): a sold-out account keeps one plotted zero — one DAY at this grain vs one month-end at the monthly's — then ends, with `deposits_acum` clipped alongside its value exactly as `applyTrailingZeroTailClipToBlock` bundles them. **Display-only**: bucket `points` (the P/L legs, which read the untouched `wealthByAccount`) keep their true zeros. Residual by construction: the monthly's kept zero lands on a month-end and the daily's a day earlier, so one cell per sold-out account reads monthly-0 vs daily-null at month-end comparisons. **AFP:** its stored historical rows are a **write-once cache no scheduled job refreshes** (the `afp_uno` sync only upserts a today spot row via `upsertAfpSpotValuationWithExplicitPx`; `revalueAfpAccountFromCuotas` is manual, its script `afp-uno-apply-valuation` in `server/scripts/archive/`) — after a ledger rebuild, revalue the stored rows first, then trust the chart. Each fund manager is its own account with its own official series (see «The AFP account's ledger and price»), so ledger × price values every date and the stored rows are re-valued from it (they still carry `units_snapshot` readers); the live last point is patched by `patchLiveLastPoint` in `valuationTimeseries.ts`.
- **Dashboard «Patrimonio neto» daily mode + the USD milestone lines:** in Período Diario the home net-worth chart swaps to the daily series. There is no «Cuentas principales» chart (no `accounts_ex_property` / `primary_lines` payloads) and no separate patrimonio-vs-invested milestone chart. **The USD milestone reference lines (US$50k … 500k) sit on the «Patrimonio neto» overview chart:** the home overview block (`withUsdMilestoneReferences`, home builder only — nav pages keep the plain overview) carries `usd_*` fields on every point, the five `reference` lines and `referenceMilestoneByDate` for the client's leading anchors, and each overview-daily point carries the same fields (`milestoneFieldsForDate`). Milestones are the USD amount in the request unit — flat in USD, US$ × the day's fx in CLP, and a unit with a direct USD rate (EUR/USD) converts in `usdMilestoneLevelInUnit`, never crossing through CLP. The client draws them faint (`isFaintReference`, the `dashboard-overview` color plan, keyed on `level_currency`): they never stretch the Y axis and are clipped to it, stay out of the legend and take no hover (no hit line); each carries `level_currency: "usd"` (the currency its level is fixed in), and the tooltip lists it only when the chart shows another unit; the chart-shape skeleton carries them too, so loading and loaded draw the same lines. The monthly totals are mark-based, so the daily and monthly overview agree to the peso at month-ends (`dashboardOverviewDaily.test.ts`).
- **One home Δ chart, stacked by class.** «PL inversiones» stacks the retiro + brokerage P/L (class colours) with a «Σ Δ mensual» line over the stack; no deposits; the YTD area always shows, and the «Total» toggle adds the all-time P/L (`accumulated_earnings`) as a solid line. The whole combo is served (see «One month series per account»): the page bundle's `inversionesPerf`, monthly and yearly only (no day mode; it offers the Rangos from 1 año, `LONG_RANGE_OPTIONS`, and a stored day Rango reads as its default, `longSurfaceRange` — as does the home «Flujos y PL» chart). `MonthlyPerformanceComboChart` takes a bar `stackId`: stacked bars draw with `stackOffset="sign"`, the Y axis sums each stack's positive and negative parts, and the bar band counts one slot per stack; charts without a stack id are unaffected. **A «%» toggle (surface `home.perf`)** switches it to returns: each class's own return per period as side-by-side bars (`pct_retirement`, `pct_brokerage` — they do not add up, so they do not stack), Inversiones' return as the Σ line (`pct_combined`), YTD % as the area (`ytd_pct`), all-time % for «Total» (`accumulated_pct`). All from the one % rule, server-built on every point, plus `yearly_pct` on the monthly block (one point per calendar year: a year's % is a compound, so the client takes it instead of rolling months up; a Rango clips whole years in this view).
- **Monthly dashboard chart totals = marks at month-ends:** the dashboard overview chart values each chart date by **Σ `accountMarkClpAtYmd`** at that date (`slugMarkTotalsAtDatesClp` in `server/src/dashboardChartMarkTotals.ts`), the same marks the daily views sum — so a monthly chart value at date `d` equals the daily chart value at `d` by construction. `buildDashboardPortfolioGroupTotalsClp` reads the consolidated monthly closings for **date discovery only** (which month-ends/snapshot dates are chart points — the P/L detalle tables keep using them) and then re-values those dates through marks; don't forward-fill monthly closings onto chart dates here. Overview slugs (real_estate/retirement/brokerage/cash_eqs/liabilities) reproduce the daily overview builder exactly (same accounts, same cash CC-netting; chart values are exact, never rounded). The **headline/cards path** (`buildDashboardBucketValueTotalsInUnit` in `portfolioGroupValueAtDate.ts`: each bucket Σ its members' values, `bucketValueInUnitAtRaw`; net worth rounded once to the unit's coin and the buckets apportioned to it) agrees with the chart's last point (`dashboardBucketsTodayInUnit`, the same sums before the rounding) to under a coin, because both value today through marks. Marks are read at the month-ends only — NOT a full daily grid, which makes the build far slower.
- **One node-value rule (`nodeValue.ts`).** A nav node's value at a date in a unit is Σ its member accounts' values in that unit, less the linked cards' owed when the node nets them; every reader passes a `NodeValuePolicy` (`{ netsLinkedCards }`) through `nodeValue` / `nodeValuesAtDates` / `nodeValueSeriesOnGrid` / `nodeValueSeriesFromSums`. The netting is declared on one node (`cash_savings`, migration 336; `nodeNetsLinkedCards`), and a net-worth bucket nets through the declaring node inside it (`nettingGroupOf` in `navTreeModel.ts`). A node with no member value that day is null, except a netting node, which reads 0 less the cards' owed; linked cards that owe with no rate into the unit that day throw. **`cashNetOfLinkedCreditCards` is the only place the linked-card total enters a cash value** — the live card and its prior closes, the daily series, the monthly chart totals, the group consolidation and the daily reference overlays (the client never re-sums it, and never converts it: a placeholder is always its own unit's `nw_bucket_totals`). Signed, never clamped: an overpaid card owes a negative amount, so a linked total in credit adds to cash, as Pasivos counts it as negative debt; the linked-balance footer shows it (hidden only at 0; `cashSavingsLinkedBalances` in `cashEqsBucketNet.ts`). Node values are exact, never rounded: where a total sits beside its parts (the headline and its bucket cards today and at the day / month / year closes, the allocation rows, the value map via `settleMapToCoins`) the total is rounded to the unit's coin and the parts apportioned to it (`apportionToCoin` in `coinApportionment.ts`: largest remainder, null parts take no share, throws on a total the parts do not round to); a chart point is never apportioned and P/L inputs stay unrounded. Don't clamp on one path only: the live card and its prior-day close would differ by the credit (a phantom day change).
- **Day period view:** a surface's period (`SurfacePeriod`, `surfaceDisplayPrefs.ts`) is `"day" | "month" | "year"`. Dashboard rows carry `deposits_day` / `delta_day` / `prior_day_close` (in the payload's unit), anchored at **plain yesterday (Chile calendar) for every account** (marks are flat on each account's closed days, so per-account market calendars fall out of the level delta with no anchor table). Both liabilities keep the loss-negative convention `prior − close − payments` (CC `delta_day` = −that day's financing charges; see the CC financing-P/L bullet). Crypto rows mark **live** — `getAccountPositionMeta`'s crypto branch is live-first like `equityBrokeragePositionMeta` (a raw-EOD position value would pin rows at yesterday's close and zero the day delta, since the calendar-day anchor mark IS that close). Bucket totals carry `prior_closes.day` + `day_end` (`buildDashboardNwBucketTotals`: the one headline function at yesterday, the prior month-end and the prior year-end), and `dashboardNavCardMetrics` emits a `day` variant per node (the inversiones hub composes day from child buckets — the consolidated slice is monthly). - **A managed portfolio is one bucket under Acciones (migrations 190, 192–194, `accountPlacement.ts`).** A broker-managed portfolio (Racional «Portafolio IPSA»: ETF + the caja its 1 %/yr fee is charged from as `cash_fee`) is a brokerage bucket with its OWN asset group, members' leaf asset groups **reparented** under it — leaf slugs and behavior kinds (`accountBucketKindSlug`) unchanged; `portfolioCajaClpAccountId`'s `brokerage_cash__caja_*` glob still finds the caja. Filed apart the fee reads as a huge loss on a tiny cash account; together the % is (holding P/L − fee) ÷ portfolio. It sits under `brokerage_acciones` as «IPSA» (`/inversiones/brokerage/acciones/ipsa`, slug `brokerage_portafolio_ipsa`, members «CFIETFIPSA», «Caja IPSA»). **Portfolio groups may hold accounts beside sub-buckets** (credit-card and liability trees keep «sub-groups XOR accounts»): `assertNavGroupItemsShape` requires each account in ≤ 1 group and linked groups / expense places unmixed; it runs in the seed transaction (violation rolls back). Placement files an account under the bucket backed by the nearest asset group above its leaf, and links a data-defined bucket (backed by its own asset group, filed under Brokerage or under another bucket) to its parent while it holds something. A sub-bucket beside accounts reads as a leaf of that page: one line per first-level child in Agrupado by balance (`mixedChartChildren`; Sin agrupar opens it), one table row with server card metrics and `card_metrics_by_slug[slug].row_pct`, one breakdown line, a non-expanding sidebar entry. The Panel offers a bucket as account home when it has no sub-buckets or already holds accounts (`resolveBucketParentAssetSlug`, `listLeafPortfolioGroupBuckets`). A new portfolio = asset group + leaf reparent + `portfolio_groups` row, no code; nav label = data label. Caveat: the Racional fee import needs exactly one `brokerage_cash__caja_*` account; a second portfolio needs that lookup keyed on the bucket.
- **Broker cash balances are cash, not investments (migration 241).** Racional CLP (`import:panel|kind=clp|key=clp`) and USD (`…|kind=usd|key=usd`) sit under Efectivo › Cash savings (`cash_eqs__cash_savings__racional_clp__clp` / `…_racional_usd__usd`), like Fintual's; no Brokerage › Cash bucket exists (demo: `cash_eqs__cash_savings__usd`). Portfolio cajas keep `brokerage_cash__caja_*` under the unit's asset group (matched by slug). So a `stock_buy` from Racional CLP is an aporte into Brokerage and sale proceeds a withdrawal. `accountUsesEquityMtm` must exempt CLP cash as it does USD cash (the `stock_sell` proceeds leg is the wallet).
- **Panel / nav / dashboard** share the tree: `GET /api/meta/sidebar-nav` → `net_worth`.

## Group page charts — server-side "Agrupado" bucketing

**Grouped chart series come from the server; the client never re-aggregates.** `server/src/groupChartBuckets.ts` (bucket selection ported from the sidebar `NavTreeNodeDto` walk) emits sibling fields on existing responses, so the toggle is instant:

- **Valuation** (`getGroupValuationTimeseries(..., { groupedBlocks: true })`, `valuationTimeseries.ts`): `nav_grouped_blocks: { grouped, ungrouped }` + `nav_grouped_proportional`, or `liab_grouped_block` + `liab_grouped_proportional` (Pasivos, no toggle). Built from the **pre-clip consolidated** block with `__group_val_total` / `__group_dep_total` (and Total) copied **verbatim**, then each block display-clipped independently.
- **Monthly P/L bars** (`getGroupMonthlyPlSeries(..., { groupedBlocks: true })`, `groupMonthlyPlSeries.ts`): `nav_grouped_bars` / `liab_grouped_bars`, keys `pl_nav_<slug>` / `pl_liab_<slug>` = per-row sums of member `pl_<id>`; unbucketed accounts keep their bar, so bars add to `delta_total`.
- Only group HTTP routes pass `groupedBlocks: true`. Client `groupPageChartViews.ts` only **picks** by toggle (`resolveGroupPageChartContext` reads toggle visibility from payload presence).
- **Pasivos buckets** read each liability node's `member_account_ids` (incl. retired cards); a card bucket is the card — never name heuristics.
- Bucket labels carry `name_i18n_key` (translate at render, never cache `t()`; deposit-line keys `charts.groupAccumulatedDeposits` / `charts.accumulatedDeposits`).

**Tail clip is display-only** (`timeseriesTailClip.ts`, final step): nulls sold-out trailing series and clips each line's aportes with its value, but **never** rewrites an aggregate. `__group_val_total` is the only line excluded from deposit bundling; `__group_val_total` / `__group_dep_total` are never recomputed post-clip. Don't Σ clipped series on the client (totals would disagree with the summary card).

## Rentabilidad benchmark row (migration 207, `benchmarkLevels.ts`, `benchmarkComparison.ts`)

- **One definition of a period's % (`periodPctDailyChain.ts`).** Every % the app shows is the scope's daily flow-adjusted `pct` (its own daily series) compounded over the period — a day is one step; 1W, a month, a year, YTD, the 1A/3A/5A windows and the all-time Total compound the days in them; a day with no return is flat, a period with none null. Readers: the Rentabilidad cells, 1D/1W included (`withDailyChainedReturns`, applied after `withShortHorizonCells`); the «Detalle por mes / año» rows of accounts (`monthlyPerfForAccountDisplay`) and groups (`withGroupDailyChainedMonthPct`, `detalleDailyChainedPct.ts`; a year is the compound of its months, so of its days); the dashboard account rows' `pct_*` (`withDailyChainedAccountRowPcts`) and the nav groups' `row_pct` / `parent_pct` (`withChainedGroupPct`, the accounts table and the value map's colours); the home class Δ chart's % view. Don't apply the one-step `flowAdjustedPct` at a surface's own grain: it counts a whole month's or year's flow from its first day, so card, Rentabilidad and detalle disagree whenever money moves mid-period. Kept on the one-step rule: a scope of liabilities only (its P/L is a cost, not a return; Pasivos pages, card and mortgage rows) and the legacy group sub-tab. P/L acumulado ÷ net deposits rides beside the compounded Total (`pct_total_on_deposits` on account rows, `on_deposits` on a group's total cell) and shows as the Total %'s tooltip. Every non-liability account and portfolio group therefore needs its full-history daily series in both units; `rentabilidadDailyWarm.ts` warms all of them. **The chains keep only each day's pct (`dailyPctSeries.ts`):** `groupDailyPctPoints` / `accountDailyPctPoints` / `dailyPctPointsForScope` build the full series uncached (its past part still from the history tier) and cache `{as_of_date, pct}` per day under the daily-series family (`daily.series|<scope>|<unit>|0|pct|…`); the full payload is built and cached only when a page's day view asks for it. Don't make % readers hold the full cached payload: it exhausts the heap (the demo's Render Starter instance, ~256 MB V8 heap, crash-looped on it). The warm's log line prints heap used and limit; `scripts/profile-cache-memory.ts` lists what each warm step retains and the largest keys; `render.yaml` starts the server with `NODE_OPTIONS=--max-old-space-size=320` on the start command, not as a service env var (that also reaches the build, and the client's Vite build aborts at 320 MB). Parity: `inversionesPerfPct.test.ts`, `dailyPctSeries.test.ts`.
- **The month windows (MTD → TOTAL) are chained from DAILY returns** (`periodReturnsDaily.ts`): each day's flow-adjusted `pct` of the page's own daily series (`resolveAccountDailySeries` / `resolveGroupDailySeries`, full history), from the month-end before the window's first month through today; the pesos and the window bounds stay the monthly rows'. (Monthly chaining counts a month's deposits from its first day and misstates months with large mid-month flows.) The full-history daily series behind every Rentabilidad table are warmed in CLP after the page bundles (`rentabilidadDailyWarm.ts`, one scope per macrotask).
- **Every Rentabilidad list (dashboard, account pages, investment group pages, Inversiones) compares each period with a selectable benchmark** (the «vs <benchmark> ▾» picker in its heading row; the list's shape is in `client-ui.md`), default the mortgage, choice remembered per surface (`<pageKey>.returns`, `useSurfaceBenchmark`). Fetched apart from the page payloads (`GET /api/benchmark-comparison?benchmark=&unit=&account_id=|portfolio_group=`, `GET /api/benchmarks`), so a change of benchmark refetches only the benchmark's figures. A list keeps every period carrying a % or a P/L of its own and prints «—» for a null figure once its payload is in (blank only while loading); a list with no such period says so in one line.
- **The row is a shadow portfolio:** for each window it starts with what the account or group was worth at the window start (1D/1W: the marks on the anchor day; month windows: the first month's `prior_closing`, the figure the real P/L starts from) and receives the same flows on the same days (`loadMergedDisplayDepositInflowEvents`, `flowEventInUnit` — transfers between a group's members cancel), all in the benchmark. Cells: the benchmark's own return over the window (L(end) ÷ L(start) − 1, comparable with the real time-weighted %), the shadow P/L, and Δ = the real cell's P/L − the shadow's. A window that starts empty measures from its first flow; a benchmark with no level at the start or on a flow day leaves the cell empty, never a guess. Levels are in the display unit at each day's rate (live fx today).
- **Benchmarks are rows of `benchmarks`, one per kind:** `equity_with_dividends` (`equity_daily` closes, each `equity_dividends` dividend reinvested at its ex-date close net of `withholding_pct`; SPY at 15%), `fund_unit` (a `fund_unit_daily` series; Risky Norris = `fintual_cert_risky_norris`), `index_plus_rate` (UF compounded at `rate_pct`: the mortgage at 4,95%, UF at 0, a DAP at 2% — migration 208). A level read on or before its date throws when the observation is over 10 days old (a stale series, not a weekend). A new benchmark of an existing kind is a row, not code. **`portfolio_group` (migration 209)** names one of the user's own groups (`portfolio_group_slug`; seeded Brokerage, Acciones, APV, Crypto): its level is the group's time-weighted return, each day's flow-adjusted `pct` from its own daily series chained (`chainDailyReturns`), in the display unit; offered under «Tus portafolios» in both selectors and only while the group exists. It is chained DAILY, so it differs from the group's Rentabilidad TOTAL, which chains monthly returns (every month's flows counted from day 1). In the prepayments comparison, payments that predate a benchmark's history are listed without an invested value and left out of both sides of the totals (`covered_payments` / `covered_from`).
- **`equity_dividends` is write-once** (Yahoo `events=div`): `stocks_nyse` fetches it with the closes for every NYSE ticker and benchmark tickers are always in that sync; a restated amount is a sync note, never overwritten. History: `npm run backfill:equity-dividends -w nw-tracker-server [-- --ticker=SPY --dry-run]`.
- Check: the SPY account against the SPY benchmark differs by tens of pesos over YTD/1A (ex-date vs pay-date reinvestment).
- **Both sides — yours and the benchmark's — carry the money's IRR** (`real_irr_pct` / `shadow_irr_pct`, `irr.ts`): the rate that turns the window's starting value and every flow, on its date, into the end value — yearly on windows of 365 days or more, over the window on shorter ones (`irr_annualized`; annualizing a day is noise). Solved by a grid scan plus bisection; null when no rate or several rates fit (e.g. a shadow the same withdrawals leave below zero). Don't use a Modified Dietz money-weighted %: it inflates on long windows with large flows both ways.
- **The mortgage page's «Prepagos vs invertir»** (`mortgagePrepaymentComparison.ts`, `GET /api/mortgage-prepayment-comparison?account_id=&benchmark=&unit=`) follows every peso paid above the minimum cuota (`loan_payments.extra_principal` on the `loan` rows: the `prepago` rows and the extra in each regular payment) to today two ways: prepaid, at the `mortgage` benchmark; invested the same day in the selected benchmark (default SPY, `account.<id>.prepayments`). Δ = invested − prepaid; totals carry both IRRs. Before tax; the insurance that shrinks with the balance is left out.

## Pasivos liabilities coverage (`referenceCoverage.ts`)

- **The Liabilities page (`/liabilities`) shows «Cobertura de los pasivos» beside «Upcoming payments»** (`CoverageLineChart`): each chart-host reference line («Disponible», «Disponible total») ÷ the total owed on the same date — every liability account on the page (`accountIsDebt`: the mortgage balance plus the cards' owed-on-date) — as a percent with a dashed 100% line (where that money would clear every debt). The cards are netted inside «Disponible» through cash, so they sit on both sides — deliberate: the chart reads «what I have against everything I owe». A date without positive debt has no point. **The axis is capped at 200% (`COVERAGE_AXIS_CAP_PCT`):** card-only years read in the thousands of percent and would flatten the mortgage era on a 0-anchored axis, so each line is DRAWN clamped to the cap (`|drawn` key) along the top edge, with its clamped run overlaid in a darker shade of its colour (`|capped` key, `cappedStroke`); the rows keep the true values under the series' own keys and the tooltip reads those (`mapPayload`). Not `allowDataOverflow`: without it Recharts grows the domain back to the data, and with it the line leaves the plot. **In Diario it shows its pending frame until its daily series lands** (see «A Diario chart never draws the monthly data» under «Loading states»).
- **Server-built at both grains, same block shape as the proportional ones** (`dates` + `series[].values`, fractions): the monthly group valuation payload's `reference_coverage` (from the block's own reference lines and liability lines, `liabilitiesCoverageFromBlock`) and the daily series' `reference_coverage` (from `reference_lines` and the liability accounts' daily lines). Data-driven: emitted only where the page hosts reference lines AND holds a liability account, so Efectivo (which hosts «Tarjeta de crédito») and the sub-pages keep their composition chart. It reuses that slot's Período/Rango (`liab.<slug>.proportional`).
