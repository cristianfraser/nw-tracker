## Page grid and tiles (`docs/layout-grid-plan.md`, `styles/tokens.css`, `shell.css`, `page-grid.css`, `cards.css`)

- **The shell is two tracks; only the content carries the column grid.** `.layout--with-sidebar` is `var(--sidebar-w) minmax(0, 1fr)`: the sidebar a fixed 11.625rem (sized to the widest nav label), sticky, outside the columns, its own `--sidebar-bg` (a shade above `--bg`), no border. **Its rows (`AppSidebar.tsx`, `AppSidebar.module.css`):** text sits on one column per depth (`--sidebar-text-x` + depth × `--sidebar-indent`, 8px); every net-worth group with rows under it (one that shows a chevron; a sub-bucket shown as a leaf has none) carries a 6px square in its card pill's colour (`navNodeMarker` in `sidebarNavFromApi.ts`, `mutedPillBackgroundRgb`) in the margin left of its text, and «Gastos» the expenses favicon's red down-pointing triangle (`EXPENSES_FAVICON_SPEC` in `favicon.ts`, the one definition both read), drawn in the selected chart style (`RoughSwatchBox` / `RoughSwatchTriangleDown` through `SketchMarker`, every style but plain; imported directly, as `Pill` imports `PillScribble` — lazy-loading them paints flat marks and bare pills until the chunk arrives). The open page's row has an inset rounded highlight and the groups above it read bright; a group opens/closes from a chevron in the same margin, which replaces its square on hover or keyboard focus (a group without a square shows it while closed, and always on touch; on touch the square stays and is itself the button). Every group starts open except a leaf group — one whose rows include accounts or tracked places (`holdsAccounts`, set in `sidebarNavFromApi.ts`); the viewer's open / closed choices are kept per node in localStorage (`nw-sidebar-open.v2`), and the open page's ancestors open on navigation. No carets, rails, hyphens or accent bar. An account that holds nothing today (`accountChartInactive`, dollar cash included) is not in the nav tree. Content is capped at `--page-max` (105rem from 1440, left-aligned beyond). Tokens on `:root`: `--content-cols` (4 on a phone, **12 from 880** on both desktop tiers, so a tile's span never changes between them), `--gutter` (1rem, 1.5rem from **1440**), `--row-gap` (= gutter), `--row-unit` (1.5rem, one UI line), `--margin-inline` (0.75rem phone, 1rem small desktop, 1.5rem big), derived `--content-w` / `--col-w`, plus `--hairline`. Media queries cannot read custom properties, so the two tier lines are literals wherever a stylesheet needs them; 880 is also the tables' narrow line (`useIsNarrowViewport.ts`), so the app has one small-desktop line. `.layout-main` (track 2) reserves the ticker's strip at the top and is no scroll box: the document is the only scroller, and nothing in the content may be wider than it — a table shows its cards instead (see «UI tables — desktop/mobile sync»), a row list wraps by container query. A page that scrolls sideways is the error showing; never hide it behind an overflow box (`auto`, `hidden` or `clip`). With no box, `position: sticky` inside a page sticks to the viewport — under the fixed ticker, `top: calc(var(--market-ticker-height) + 1px)` (the checking statement's head and month headers). `.layout-main>.content` is the column grid; every page's `<main>` is one child spanning it. It is also the `page-content` size container (a plain grid, so containment costs no subgrid — never make a page grid a container, size containment turns `subgrid` off): a tile whose span depends on the content's width queries it inside the 880 tier line, e.g. the position page's band, chart 7 | rail 5 while the content is at most 55rem wide (`PositionAccountDetailPage.module.css`). The ticker: on a phone a full-viewport bar and on the small desktop a bar across the content's width, both with the content reserving its strip (`.layout-main` padding-top); on the big desktop it covers the content's last 8 columns (5–12) and the content starts at the top. The prefs dock's max-width derives from the tokens.
- **Whatever a route renders is on the grid by itself:** `page-grid.css` makes the content region's direct child (a page's `<main>`, or a layout's) a subgrid of the content columns (`row-gap: var(--row-gap)`), so a page root declares nothing. A container INSIDE a page that holds tiles of its own says so with `.page-grid` (same subgrid): a Loadable shell (`GroupInfoBase`, `AccountDetailSharedLayout`, the tax return), a page inside the flows or panel layout with its own root (groceries), a tile grouping sub-tiles. Each section is a **`PageGridTile`** (`components/layout/PageGridTile.tsx`: `<section data-tile=… class="page-tile">`) with its heading INSIDE it, so the row gap is the only space between sections — no outside margins (`.page-tile>:is(h2,h3,h4,.chart-panel-title-row):first-child { margin-top: 0 }`, `.chart-panel-title-row>:is(h2,h3,h4) { margin: 0 }`; no inline `marginTop` / `margin` / `fontSize` on sections). A `.chart-grid` is a subgrid where it is a page-grid child (two panels on six columns each from 880; `--full-line` all of them) and a plain 12-column grid with the same gutter elsewhere. Chart titles and surface controls use `.chart-panel-title-row`; control styles live in `styles/controls.css`. **Account pages are on the grid (`AccountDetailSharedLayout`):** the content shell is a page grid, the strip a `page-grid` tile, and every section a tile (position, charts as `chart-grid` tiles, returns, detalle, cartola months, each credit-card table, mortgage form / sheet / scenarios / prepayments, capability forms, flows); a section made of several tiles (the card's cuotas block, the depto ledger block) is a `page-grid` container with one dim marker. The credit-card group page is the exception to the asset-group layout: it renders no strip and no valuation / share charts, only its own lanes, «Upcoming payments» beside the card «History», and the monthly detail (`CreditCardGroupSection`, see «The credit-card group page reads like a card page» in cards.md); its lanes (`CreditCardGroupLanes`: card | owed | cupo | date lane, that order in the markup) and the upcoming list (`CcUpcomingPaymentsList`) are row lists, one rendering at every width (container queries: below 44rem a lane drops to a line of its own under its card with its own date labels, below 26rem the cupo moves under the owed figure; below 24rem an upcoming month's bar takes its own line). From 880, with «History» beside it, the upcoming list is as tall as the chart (size containment) and scrolls vertically inside its panel, like the dashboard rail's «Últimos movimientos»; on one column it keeps its own height and never scrolls. **The mortgage group page is its own page** (`MortgageGroupPage`, route `/liabilities/mortgage`, not `GroupInfoBase`): the mortgage page's header (hero = the loans' balance in the display unit, día / mes / año / total financing cost) over the `depto.scope` page grid — a «next payment(s)» line, the loan list (a row list, one rendering at every width: wide rows are subgrids under a header, below 44rem of its frame each loan is four lines whose figures name themselves, in DOM order (next cuota left of the rate); balance and next cuota in the loan's own currency with the unit under them, paid bar, rate, payoff at the usual pace, a total row with several loans; each loan links to its page) and the balance band (the loans' balances stacked in the unit beside the rail: paid / left / prepaid, financing cost by year). Then «Dividendos»: every loan's payments as ONE list (`DeptoPaymentsList`, the loan page's component, fed the server's merge `payments`: each loan's next cuota, one Σ row, all payments newest first keyed `<account id>:<label>` on the largest bar scale, a loan swatch on each row when there are several; loans in different currencies get a note instead, `payments_mixed_currencies`; its prepaid-vs-invested column from `GET /api/portfolio-groups/:slug/mortgage-prepayments`, the loans' comparisons merged and totalled by the same rule, `prepaymentTotals`). No composition chart and no returns table; the payoff projection and recording a payment stay on each loan's page. On the big desktop the header's strip pads below the ticker (the mortgage page relies on its second hero line for that). **Every net-worth group page under `/inversiones/*` and `/cash_eqs/*` is the glance page** (`GroupInfoPage` → `InvestmentGroupPage`, or `BasketGroupPage` for a basket; there is no generic group page): the server marks the nav node (`investment_glance`, `cash_glance`, `basket`) and builds `group_glance` on its nav context (`groupGlance.ts`). **A cash group (`cash_glance`: a group of the cash bucket, `isCashGroup`) is the same page in its cash form** (`group_glance.kind` "cash"): the title's value is the node-value rule's (its accounts less the linked cards where the group nets them); the band chart is «Saldo» without the aportes line; the strip is [balance by currency — each currency's balance in that currency and in the unit, and what the linked cards owe — | the change of the balance over the day, month and year, on the period bars]; the table has no % / P/L / aportes columns and no Performance toggle — name, one-year balance line, the balance in the currency the accounts hold (one line per currency for a group), the value in the unit, weight, month and year change — with the linked cards as a negative row of their own, last, linking to their page (only where the group nets them: the bucket and the netting node, not Checking), and keeps name, own-currency balance and value when narrow; «Saldo por cuenta» (the stacked chart, no aportes line) takes the pair's row alone; no «P/L mensual», no Rentabilidad list; Detalle spans the page; movements and export as on the investment page. **The real-estate group page is the property page for every property** (`RealEstateGroupPage`, route `/real_estate`, not `GroupInfoBase`; payload `GET /api/portfolio-groups/:slug/real-estate-glance?unit=`, `realEstateGroupGlance.ts`, built from each property's own `depto_glance`): with one property the payload is that property's page block untouched (`single`) and the page renders the property page's header (`DeptoHeaderView`: loan-currency hero over the unit) and bands (`DeptoPropertyBands`, no «movimientos») under the group's title, so the two pages show the same figures. With several (`several`) the hero is their net equity in the display unit with «N properties» under it and the strip their strips added (día unknown when any property has none; año has no %); then the property list (`RealEstatePropertyList`, a row list, one rendering at every width: each property's own header as a row — swatch, name linking to its page, net equity in its loan currency over the unit, the share of its appraisal it owns, día / mes / año / total, a figure's % under it; below 52rem of its frame four lines whose figures name themselves, and while wide each figure keeps its label for screen readers; a property with no ledger listed without figures and in no sum) and the property page's three bands over the server's sums: value (`RealEstateGroupValueChart`: net equity stacked by property in the rows' colours under the total's line, the appraisals dashed, owed as the band between, deposited thin; the rail's owned | owed bar, appraisal, purchase value and revaluation, pies, labelled since no own-currency figure sits beside them), P/L (`PlChart` and `PlRail` as on the property page; this month's split names the loan currencies behind its currency part) and cost of living (`CostChart` / `CostRail` over the properties' months added per kind, the 12-month window by the property page's rule; absent when no property has a tracked place; its link goes to the real-estate expenses page). No value map, valuation chart, Δ charts, monthly detail, flows list or accounts tree; rent is not on the page (the property page has none). **The Liabilities page is the two composed** (`LiabilitiesPage`, route `/liabilities`, not `GroupInfoBase`): the mortgage page's header over everything owed (`DebtGlanceHeader`, shared with the mortgage group page: total owed, «N cards · N loans», the financing-cost strip), a split bar mortgage | cards in their nav colours with each group's amount and share, and the next payments (the first month of the list below); then the credit-card group's lanes (`CreditCardGroupLanes` on `cc-lanes`, in the billing currency as on that page) and the mortgage group's loan list (`MortgageLoanList` on `mortgage-glance`), each under a title linking to its page, where the history, the monthly detail, the balance band and the payments live; then «Upcoming payments» over both (`LiabilitiesUpcomingList`, the card group's list with each loan's cuota: minimum in its colour, the room up to its maximum hatched, the month's maximum under its total) beside «Liabilities coverage». The head and the list are one payload, `GET /api/portfolio-groups/liabilities/liabilities-glance?unit=` (`liabilitiesGlance.ts`), built from the two groups' builders in the unit: the cards' owed = the lanes' total, each currency at today's debt rate; the loans' balance = the mortgage glance's; the strip = the mortgage glance's plus the cards' dashboard rows (within the cards, a period a card has no figure for is left out, null when none has it; across the two groups, a period either lacks is null, never a partial sum); the months = each card's month sum from the lanes (`by_card`) plus each loan's cuotas as «Próximos pagos» lists them (`mortgageUpcomingMonthsByLoan`), over that panel's horizon at today's mid rate, an earlier month folded into the current one. Its sections hide when the payload says the group is absent. No accounts tree, valuation chart or account table.
- **A card's title is a pill in its bucket's colour (`DashboardCardTitleRow` `colorRgb`):** the nav node's own `color_rgb` (the colour its chart lines use, set from the panel), drawn with the shared `Pill` on a muted version of it (`mutedPillBackgroundRgb`: lightness ×0.9, chroma ×0.8 in OKLCH); small, not uppercase, the text the pill's own colour with only its lightness moved (`tintedTextOnRgb`: dark on a light pill, light on a dark one). A card with no colour keeps plain text.
- **Card numbers flash on a change (trial; `numberChangeEffect.ts`, `AnimatedNumberFlow` `flashOnChange`):** when a strip card's balance or breakdown line changes in the same display unit after a settled render, the digits switch at once and the cell flashes green or red by direction (`flashOnChange`): instant on, ~200 ms hold, 2 s fade; a new change restarts it. The four P/L figures and the summary cards' balances (the page's own total in row 1 and an account page's hero, `CompactEntityCard`) do not flash; they keep the rolling digits. A currency switch, or a dimmed placeholder giving way to live figures, keeps the rolling digits. `NUMBER_CHANGE_FLASH_ENABLED = false` brings the rolling digits back everywhere.
- **Cards have no chrome and touch** (no padding, background, border or radius, no inset yet — aligning card text with the container edge is the next step; `--card-radius` only skins popovers and the modal). A strip is `.cards-strip` (`PortfolioEntityCardsStrip`: one `.cards.cards--summary` block over one `.cards.cards--detail` block; the accounts table a sibling `.cards-strip__table`), a subgrid of the content columns; each `.cards` block is **four equal columns of its own** across the strip with no gap (not a subgrid of the page columns: the gutters would split across the cards and make the outer ones narrower, misaligning the rows). A row the cards do not fill closes with a line just outside its last card (`box-shadow`), on the pixel the next row's card draws its left border on; dividers are per-card borders stripped by a **fixed 4-up count** (`nth-child(4n+1)` no left border, `nth-child(n+5)` a top border; never auto-fill), the one line between the blocks the detail block's border-top. The summary row is the same 4-up block with two cells empty. Below 880 cards are 1-up, 2-up between 600 and 879. A bare `.cards` outside a strip (the flows periods, the mortgage / credit-card summary rows) is a plain 4-column grid with the same dividers. `.chart-box`, `.chart-box--pending` and `.table-wrap` have no chrome (the pending chart frame is the title row plus the reserved height).
- **The dashboard's big-desktop rail (≥1440):** `GroupInfoBase` takes `rail`, `pairs` and `allocation`; with a rail the strip and `charts` (the net-worth chart) go into `.page-main` (columns 1–8) and the rail tiles into `.page-rail` (9–12, pulled back one gutter so the two stacks touch), two independent column stacks (never shared explicit rows: a tall rail table would open whitespace in the main stack). The rail stretches to the band's row and its last tile, «Últimos movimientos» (`.dashboard-rail-fill`), takes what the rail's other tiles leave: its rows scroll under the fixed heading in a size-contained box (`.dashboard-rail-fill__scroll`, `contain: size`, `overflow-y: auto`, never under 12rem), so they add nothing to the row's height. The band is therefore as tall as the main stack — the net-worth chart and «Últimos movimientos» end on the same line — unless «Flujos», «Próximos pagos», the list's heading and its 12rem floor need more; then the band grows to them, so the rail never spills onto the row below. Below 1440 the list is not a scroll box. Below the band the tiles take their span from the layout table (`PageGridTile` `wideSpan` → `--tile-span-l`, `dashboardTileWideSpan`): flows chart | Δ by class, then the allocation stack | the detalle table, then the wide tables, then the export row and the accounts tree last, full width on every tier (the export row always right above the tree). **The allocation stack** (`ALLOCATION_STACK` in `client/src/dashboardLayout.ts`, `.dashboard-tile-stack` in `page-grid.css`): the allocation map with Rentabilidad right under it, at every width — from 880 one half-width column of its own rows beside the detalle table, below 880 two tiles in the stack; Rentabilidad is left out when the server sends no returns. **Nothing reorders tiles** (no CSS `order`): the drawn order is the DOM order at every width, read column by column where stacks sit side by side, so focus and screen readers follow what is drawn. Below 1440 `.page-main` / `.page-rail` are `display: contents` and the tiles are page-grid items in DOM order (`DASHBOARD_READING_ORDER`): strip, net-worth chart, «Flujos» periods, «Próximos pagos», «Últimos movimientos», flows chart, Δ by class, allocation, Rentabilidad, detalle, wealth flows, export, accounts — so the rail's panels sit right under the chart on a phone. `dashboardLayout.ts` is the one table of tile rectangles per tier in react-grid-layout's own shape (`GRID` is its `Responsive` props); `dashboardLayout.test.ts` asserts that each tier's placement reads in that order, the spans and the big-desktop placement. In the rail a `.cards` block is 2-up (a `.cards--list` block — «Próximos pagos» — stays one card per row there and at 600–879; a `.cards--pair` block — the «Flujos» periods — stays 2-up below 600 too) and a parallel table shows its mobile rendering by the rail's width (`@container rail (max-width: 40rem)`). The home «Flujos» section is two tiles: `DashboardFlowsPeriods` (rail) and `DashboardFlowsChart` (main), on the same react-query entry. «Flujos» periods and «Próximos pagos» share one wrapper (`.dashboard-flows-pair`, `page-grid.css`): one row whenever both fit at any width — the periods at their own width, «Próximos pagos» taking the rest down to 19rem — else stacked (a narrow phone, the rail). On the small desktop (880–1439) the tiles side by side on the big desktop share a row, half the columns each: flows chart | Δ by class (`SMALL_PAIRS`, `PageGridTile` `smallSpan`, `GroupInfoBase` `tileSmallSpan`) and the allocation stack | detalle — both sides render together, never an empty half; the rest spans the row. A flows chart with no Δ by class beside it takes the whole row on both desktop tiers (`DashboardFlowsChart` `alone`). Group pages have no rail (one stack, same tiles). **The detalle tables are row lists, one rendering at every width (`PerfDetailRows`):** monthly / yearly (`MonthlyPerfDetailTable`) and daily (`DailyPerfDetailTable`) rows read by symbols, not column labels (each figure names itself on hover): flows by an icon each in the text colour — expenses a shopping bag, income two stacked banknotes, contributions a coin stack with a leaning coin (the piggy bank is savings, on the home «Flujos» panel) — with the signed amount (negatives in parentheses) giving the direction; the net-worth rows' income and expenses are `income` / `expenses` on `GET /api/groups/net_worth/consolidated-monthly?installment_mode=`, from the flows overview's months; an account's units keep a → / ← arrow, the result ▲ / ▼ with its % in the sign's colour, YTD and Σ quieter, the YTD with its % in parentheses where the row carries the server's `ytd_pct` (monthly, yearly, and daily rows fetched with `perf_levels`). While every row fits on one line the rows are subgrids of the list, one column per figure; below the list's breakpoint (a container query on the list's frame, 62rem for three flow slots, 50rem otherwise — never a measurement: measuring the rendered rows after every render flips them on each refetch and makes Firefox flash the page scrollbar) a row is three lines: the period; the closing with the result beside it, YTD on the right; the flows (expenses, income, contribution), Σ on the right — on a phone YTD and Σ share a fourth line. The result's % is in parentheses. No desktop table beside mobile cards.

- **The accounts tree («Cuentas», `components/nav/NavAccountsTree.tsx`) is a collapsible nested list** on the dashboard, the group and Pasivos pages and the panel: the server's nav tree as `ul > li > ul`, each level one step in on a guide rail; a group row is a chevron button (`aria-expanded` / `aria-controls`; a closed level stays in the DOM, `hidden`), a square in its `color_rgb`, the name as the link to its page and the slug in grey monospace (hidden under 30rem of the tree's own container); an account reads «#id name», linked to its page, and a tracked place (`exp.<slug>`) its name, linked to its expenses page — both leaves. The root and every group whose children are all groups start open, one holding accounts (a leaf group, or one mixing accounts and sub-groups) closed; the viewer's choices, and «Expandir todo / Plegar todo» (the root stays open), are kept per tree root in localStorage (`nw-tracker.accountsTree.v2.<root slug>`). No counts on the rows. Long names end in «…», never wrap or widen the page.

## Per-surface display controls

- **Período/Rango are per-surface, not global.** Every chart owns a compact Período (D/M/Y) + Rango control and every perf/flows period table a Período-only control (tables always cover FULL history and paginate at every grain). State: `client/src/surfaceDisplayPrefs.ts` — `useSurfacePrefs(surfaceId, defaultPeriod, defaultRange)` persists partial `{period, range}` JSON under `nw-tracker.surface.<pageKey>.<surfaceKey>` (missing field = caller default; per page instance; deliberately no cross-tab sync). UI: `components/ui/SurfaceControls.tsx` in `ChartPanelTitleRow`/heading rows — one rendering everywhere, a row of short text buttons per set («Composición Mapa», «D M A», «30d 60d 90d 1a 3a 5a 10a total»), the current one underlined; no selects. Each button names itself in full (`aria-label` / `title`: «Daily», «Last year», `surfaceControls.rangeName.*`) and has a 25px-tall hit area (a `::before` 6px above and below, 5px to each side, so neighbours never overlap). Rangos are `TIME_RANGE_OPTIONS` (`timeRange.ts`; a stored value no longer offered falls back to the surface default); the home net-worth chart and the M/Y-only home charts («Flujos y PL», «PL inversiones») offer only a year and up (`LONG_RANGE_OPTIONS`, a stored day window read as the default by `longSurfaceRange`). The global toolbar (`AppDisplayPreferencesBar`) carries the display unit only; the old `nw-tracker.metricsPeriod`/`.timeRange` keys are cleared at boot. Bullets elsewhere that say "toolbar Período/Rango" mean this per-surface pair.
- **Defaults**: home value charts + group/account/Pasivos valuation = Diario+3y; every P/L combo, flows chart and the CC charts = Mensual+3y (the CC ledger surfaces are independent controls — `CreditCardLedgerSurfaces.tsx`, one implementation for the card page (`cc.<id>.*`) and the Pasivos / credit-card group section (`liab.cc.<slug>.*`): `.historial` is D/M/Y + Rango and the `.detalle` table M/Y period only; the group pages get Diario through the daily-series CC block summed over the merged ledger's masters, see «CC per-day owed + daily extras»); tables = Mensual (+ full history; the home detalle table offers M/Y only, `GroupInfoDashboardOptions.detailPeriods`); proportional charts = Mensual+Todo (the Liabilities page's coverage chart, on the root's `liab.liabilities.proportional`, Mensual+3y); rates = range-only 1y. **A Rango of N days is N days ending today, today included, on both sides:** `timeRangeCutoffYmd` = today − (N − 1), the same as the server's day windows (daily series points, flows overview day rows). Every client clip goes through `clipPointsToTimeRange` / `clipRowsToTimeRange` (no inline `>= cutoff` filters).
- **Axes.** Every value chart's Y axis starts at 0 for non-negative data and spans 0 for mixed-sign data — one helper, `buildNiceYAxis` (`client/src/components/charts/chartLayout.ts`). Two deliberate exceptions: the Rates page keeps `buildNiceYAxisPositiveBand` (a quote series is about the move; a 0-anchored UF axis is a flat line) and the wealth-percentile top-% axis is a reversed log scale that cannot contain 0. X ticks are **phased on January**: month strides are divisors of 12 (1/2/3/4/6) walked from the first month index that is a multiple of the stride, so a 4-month stride reads ene/may/sep whatever month the series starts in; the day-grid helper phases its month-first ticks the same way, and multi-year strides land on round years (even years for 2, 2015/2020/2025 for 5). Only the first-data-point push (year branch) and the appended last point may sit off-phase. Known residue: on a daily grid whose first month is partial, a tick that phases onto that month sits on the first grid day, not the 1st (`computeRegularMonthXAxisTicks` cannot tell a daily grid from a month-end series with a live last point).
- **Cards show all three periods at once** (no card period anywhere): metrics grid = Mes and Año deposits + P/L rows, a divider, then the dimmed Total row (lifetime deposits + P/L); the Día P/L sits on the balance row beside the main value, with no day deposits figure (`DashboardCardGroupMetrics.tsx`); then breakdown. There is no title balance-Δ chip / `title_delta`. Chart-inactive visibility = any-period activity.
- **Composition charts are proportional** (100%-stacked areas at every grain, own controls; no pies): server-built share series in `server/src/proportionalSeries.ts` — per date base = Σ max(value, 0) over member lines (negatives floor to 0, base 0 → null; Σ shares == 1) — emitted pre-clip as `group_allocation_proportional` + `nav_grouped_proportional`/`liab_grouped_proportional` (group payloads), `allocation_proportional` (home bundle + overview-daily) and `proportional`/`grouped_proportional` (`/api/daily-series`). Client renders via `ProportionalAreaChart` (shares are unit-invariant — keep-prev passes them through unscaled). **One level only:** the home chart stacks one level below the «big» buckets of the net_worth nav tree (`compositionBigBuckets`, `dashboardComposition.ts`: net_worth's children, where a `nav_bucket` without a `dashboard_bucket_slug` — Inversiones — is replaced by its own children; liability and reference groups skipped). Its lines are each big bucket's first-level children — a child group is one line (Σ of its `listAccountsForGroupTab` accounts), a direct account one line (name, account colour) — in nav order; label, i18n key and `color_rgb` come from the nav node and the client colours by the payload colour. Values are the dashboard's marks: monthly `slugMarkTotalsAtDatesClp` at the chart dates only (reusing the primary-lines totals for the same group), daily `accountMarkClpSeriesOnGrid` — so they agree at month-ends — computed in CLP since shares are unit-invariant. The linked credit-card netting (`cashNetOfLinkedCreditCards`) is subtracted from the line whose node declares the netting (`nodeNetsLinkedCards`, `nodeValue.ts`: the savings line, same as the value map); the builder throws unless exactly one line carries it, if an account sits in two lines, or if a bucket account is in none. A group page's chart is always its direct children (`nav_grouped_proportional` is that single block) whatever the Agrupado toggle says, a leaf page its accounts; there is no `ungrouped` proportional block. Pasivos is separate (unchanged by this rule).
- **Value map («Mapa», `navValueMap.ts`).** A finviz-style treemap in the composition chart's slot on the dashboard and on every net_worth group page with something to tile — sub-groups or accounts of its own (leaf pages too, even with a single account; Pasivos keeps its chart); a «Composición / Mapa» button set in the panel title row (stored per surface as `view` beside `<pageKey>.proportional`; the map's own Día/Mes/Año return window is `<pageKey>.map`, default Día; Rango does not apply). The server emits ONE tree, `value_map` (+ `value_map_color_bounds`), beside `card_metrics_by_slug` in the page bundle, nav-context and nav-snapshot payloads; the client picks the page's subtree by slug (`valueMapRootForPage`). Nesting is the net_worth nav tree: a group with GROUP children is a frame (header strip with name + %, when there is room), a group without is the smallest tile, which carries its accounts (`leaf_accounts`): a leaf group opens into them at any depth when it holds two or more, a single account stays the group's own tile except on the page's own first level, which always opens — the page root opens into its own accounts even with one; the client decides by depth from the page root. A mixed group (stocks beside sub-buckets) shows its own accounts as tiles beside the sub-group tiles, and those sub-groups are leaves exactly as the sidebar shows them (`mapNode` / `asLeaf` in `sidebarNavFromApi.ts`): one block, no `leaf_accounts`. Liability and reference groups are not in the map. **Additivity:** tile values are Σ of account rows' current values, each account claimed by one tile (a duplicate throws), so frames add up; the one non-row figure is the linked credit-card total, which the cash card nets (`cashNetOfLinkedCreditCards`) and whose footer sits on the savings card, so the SAVINGS tile carries it (checking is a sibling leaf group and stays raw) and the cash frame equals the cash card value. Every node carries its TRUE value, negatives included (a frame = Σ of all its children); a node worth ≤ 0 is left out of the payload but still counts in every ancestor (so a card bigger than savings + checking hides the whole cash bucket, and the root stays net worth). The client sizes a frame's area by its visible children; tooltips show the true value. The map shows whenever it is the selected view: during a unit switch or a refetch it keeps drawing the last data it had (`useHeldValueMap`), never swapping back to the composition chart. Size = value in the display unit; colour = the node's flow-adjusted % for the period (`row_pct` rule over the node's rows, a single row for an account tile; an empty member with no delta does not blank a group's %) against FIXED per-period bounds the server emits (day ±3%, month ±8%, year ±25%; null % = neutral). The layout is our own squarified treemap (`treemapLayout.ts`) — Recharts' `Treemap` cannot reserve a frame header — drawn by `ValueTreemap.tsx` with CSS tokens `--treemap-*`. Every frame keeps its header: a frame squarified smaller than its minimum (header + a body, and for a frame holding frames, room for their minimum too) is grown at its siblings' expense, and each node's area is its value at one global px-per-value plus the area its headers and padding take (re-measured over a few passes), so a tile inside a frame has the same area per value as one outside. A tile's label shows only when it fits whole (text measured on hidden spans): name over % from ~36 px tall, one row from ~22 px, else none; a header drops its % first, then shortens the name. The tooltip always shows value, % and P/L; a click goes to the node's route.
- **A group valued through one proxy basket maps as the basket's holdings (`proxyBasketBreakdown.ts`, the `proxy` input of `navValueMap.ts`).** When every positive account a node claims sits on the same proxy bucket (`proxyBucketSlugForSeriesKey` in `riskyNorrisProxyMtm.ts` — the Fintual APV accounts → `fintual_risky_norris_proxy`), the node becomes a frame of `kind: "holding"` tiles, one per ETF the fund STATES in its latest `watchlist_composite_positions` row (Fintual's tickers, e.g. SPXS, not its SPY price proxy; `price_ticker` is the Yahoo one), sized by stated weight × the sleeve's share of assets, plus a neutral «Otros» tile (`valueMap.otherSleeve`) for the non-ETF sleeve (`1 − etf_weight_sum`); accounts on the same proxy merge into one breakdown, accounts on different proxies or without one keep their own tiles, and a click goes to the node's route. A holding's colour is its time-weighted return over the days the fund HELD it inside the card period (day / month / year, the cards' anchors): each positions date resets the weights, weights step between dates, disjoint holding windows chain and days out of the basket contribute nothing. The tooltip adds the holding's contribution to the fund (plain Σ weight × daily return, unlinked) and «held N/M days»; the CLP leg goes through the observado frame, like the proxy itself. Fail-fast: a positions row in force without `etf_weight_sum`, a missing close, a non-USD price ticker or a period starting before the first recorded basket throws. Client: `holding` on the value-map DTO, tooltip rows.

Running levels are sampled at year-end, never re-summed (`rollupChartPointsByYear` `levelKeys`; `rollupPerfPointsYearly` `accumKey`). Flows payloads (`/api/flows/deposits`, `/api/flows/pl`, `/api/flows/expenses/real-estate`) carry monthly blocks only; yearly rows roll up client-side (the P/L year table too). Tables stay full history.
- **The /flows P/L page is market P/L of the deposit accounts (`FLOWS_PL_BUCKETS` in `flowsPl.ts`).** Four buckets: brokerage, retirement, cash (cash_eqs WITHOUT the accounts under Checking accounts — their P/L is the plan fee and the dollar account's fx, a gasto or cash) and real estate (the property's P/L with its mortgage interés + seguros added back — `flowsMortgageCarrying.ts` — since gastos counts them, so it reads the UF revaluation); the chart, the month/year table (desktop + mobile cards) and the per-account blocks (the property's row carries the carrying back too) all show them, Diario included (cash without checking has its own daily scope `flows.pl:cash_eqs`, the others share `pg:<slug>`). The flows overview's P/L is this builder's monthly / daily total.
- **The flows overview is built on the server (`flowsOverview.ts`, `GET /api/flows/overview?unit=clp|usd&installment_mode=split|total[&days=N]`).** Per calendar month (and day, for Diario): `income` (incl. the payroll deductions counted as gastos), `expenses` (gasto del mes of the mode; refunds negative), `savings` = income − expenses, `deposits` = the deposit events of every account NOT under Checking accounts (`accountIdsUnderCheckingAccounts`: corriente, vista, the dollar account, MACH — their own events are cash; money back into checking is negative; the linked mortgage deposit keeps its amortización), `deposits_pre_tax` (AFP/AFC cotizaciones), `card_spent` (the card lines' part of expenses, plus the amortización of a mortgage dividendo a card paid, on the depto payment day in both modes — a financing projection spreads only its gasto), `card_paid` (transfers checking → card master — `pago_tarjeta`, divisas at the pesos that left checking, the dollar account's at its dollars — less card → checking top-ups; card-to-card is not cash; `flowsOverviewCash.ts`), `cash_change` (the checking accounts' ledger change, Σ `accountMarkClpAtYmd` through the mark cache, today for the month in progress), `cash_fx` (the fx result on what the checking accounts hold in another currency than the unit — Σ over days of the holding at the end of the previous day × the day's change of its rate into the unit, the card's revaluation rule (`ccRevaluationCostByDate`) on the asset side, `checkingCashByDay` in `flowsOverviewCash.ts`: in pesos the dollar checking accounts at the sell rate, live today through `rates.ts`, in dollars the peso accounts at a peso's dollar value; one quantity and one rate per account and day serve both the value and the leg, so Δvalue = fx leg + the day's flows at the day's rate and days add up to the month; the checking accounts' own P/L, which `pl` leaves out by design), `cash_unexplained` = cash_change − (savings − deposits + card_spent − card_paid + cash_fx) — shown on /flows, never forced to zero (what is left is a checking debit in no leg, e.g. a «deposits»-tagged debit whose deposit differs, an overdraft-line payment; or a dollar flow's spread between the mid the legs count it at and the sell rate the dollars are held at), `pl` (the /flows P/L page's own builder, `buildFlowsPlChartMonthly` / `buildFlowsPlDailyChartSeries` in `flowsPl.ts`, so the two read the same total every month — the checking accounts' own P/L is out, the property's mortgage carrying is added back), `investments_change` = deposits + deposits_pre_tax + pl (= Δ value of those accounts, checked to the peso) and `net_worth_change` = savings + deposits_pre_tax + pl + cash_fx. Cash leaves when a card is paid, not when it is used: the card line reads card_spent − card_paid. Full-history `totals`, `closed.{last_month,last_12_months}` — closed months only on the Chile clock (the month in progress reads as a deficit until its salary lands on the last business day) — and `days` (Diario rows inside the `days` window) only when asked; totals and every rollup sum the base legs and re-derive the rest. The /flows overview (table + «Fx de caja» + «Sin explicar») and the home dashboard's flows card (`DashboardFlowsSummary`, surface `dashboard.flows`: Ingresos, Gastos | Ahorro, Depósitos, Tarjetas gastado − pagado, Variación de caja | P/L, Variación inversiones, Variación patrimonio — no «Fx de caja» line: its nine lines are pitched to the strip cards' divider on the big desktop, and the leg reaches the card through Variación patrimonio) only pick rows, clip them to their Rango and roll years up (`flowsOverviewRows.ts`). **The client holds no copy of the derived-field rule:** each derived figure is linear in the legs, so a yearly row and the «en el rango» total are a field-by-field Σ of the server's rows; the income page reads its month rows, day points and total from `/api/income?unit=` `aggregates` (`buildFlowsIncomeAggregates`, the overview's own per-kind income buckets), and the deposits page its day points (`chart_daily*`) and category subtotals from the server. The /flows/deposits page reads the same deposits: the checking accounts out and the mortgage carrying taken back out of the property's dividendo deposits as negative `mortgage_carrying` rows (`buildFlowsDepositsResponse(unit, { excludeCheckingAccounts, withMortgageCarrying })`; the dashboard bundle's own call keeps every account and no carrying rows), so its total is the overview's deposits + deposits_pre_tax month by month (tested), shown split as post-tax / AFP+AFC (`post_tax_total_*`, `pre_tax_total_*`, `isPreTaxDepositRow`). The carrying is one list for the three readers (`flowsMortgageCarrying.ts`: from the line that is the payment, once per link — a financing projection is skipped — on the depto payment day in both installment modes). Cached as `flows.overview|<unit>|<mode>|<days>` over a shared `flows.overview.inputs|` (income, expense lines — `flows.overview.inputs|lines`, the carrying `…|carrying|<unit>` — deposits, card payments): a live-quote tick drops only the payloads; writes and historical market data drop both, and so does any API write once answered (the change log is applied when the request finishes, `ensureCacheFresh` in `index.ts`, and drops the families that read what it wrote) — but not the queue's and run log's machine traffic (heartbeats, claims, run reports), which writes only tables the log does not cover and drops nothing; the warmer builds the CLP and USD month payloads. **Income has a `rent` bucket:** `buildFlowsIncomeAggregates` sums the income kind `rent` (a tracked place's rent) into its own bucket — `rent_clp` on the month rows, `rent` on the chart points — beside salary, severance, parent_gift and other; the income chart and month table draw it (`income.chart.rent`), the client only picks.

## Loading states (`components/ui/Loadable.tsx`, `components/charts/ChartEmptyState.tsx`)

- **No view is ever replaced by a loading screen.** Every page renders its real layout at once; while a query is pending each component renders its frame with the values it has — zero placeholders, held prior data, an empty table — and dims (`Loadable` / `loadableClass`: `.loadable.is-loading`, opacity .55, inert, `aria-busy`; nested markers never compound, and `.chart-panel-title-row` stays clickable inside a dimmed surface so a Período/Rango/view change is a new request, never blocked). The only loading text is a button label while its mutation runs; never a bare «Cargando…» paragraph, `null`, or a page re-gated on a Diario/unit change. **Eager pages, lazy charts, idle prefetch (`App.tsx`, `lazyChunks.ts`, `components/charts/lazyCharts.tsx`):** the dashboard, group, Pasivos and account pages plus the flows and panel layouts ship in the entry chunk, so a document load paints the page's own layout; the chart engine (recharts) is one lazy chunk reached only through `lazyCharts.tsx` (`chartsBundle.ts` re-exports the chart components the eager pages use), whose Suspense fallback is the chart's own frame — title row with live controls plus the pending box — so a chart slot never shows text; secondary pages stay lazy behind a `null` fallback (`v7_startTransition` keeps the previous page during in-app navigation; the flows and panel layouts wrap their `Outlet` in their own `Suspense` so a direct load keeps the h1 and tabs), and `useIdleChunkPrefetch` imports every registered chunk — charts first — in an idle callback after first paint, after which navigation and charts are synchronous (`lazyComponent` renders a loaded module directly). The entry chunk must carry no recharts. **A first-ever visit (no cached nav tree) is blank, not a message:** the sidebar shows the Net worth link and the Panel footer; the dashboard (`GroupInfoBase` `coldStripShape`) draws `PlaceholderCardsStrip` — nameless cards (blank title, value and metric rows at the real rows' height, same grid and shells) in the shape `client/src/coldPageShape.ts` states (`DASHBOARD_COLD_STRIP` 2 summary + 4 bucket cards; a guess for the paint before the tree arrives, never routed through `requireNavCardMetrics`); a page title with no name yet (`PageTitleRow`) keeps a title's height, so the account placeholder is a blank title, not «Cuenta #id».
- **The home panels paint from the browser's last copy (`queries/persistedPayloadCache.ts`).** «Flujos» (its two closed periods, per unit and expense mode), «Próximos pagos» and «Últimos movimientos» (per unit) keep their last live payload in localStorage and show it, undimmed, until the server answers, like the strip's nav snapshot, the sidebar tree and a group page's shell. One key scheme for all of them, `nw:cache:<name>:v<version>[:<scope>…]`, the version being the payload's `schema_version` (`DTO_SCHEMA_VERSIONS` in contracts) or, for a shape the client builds itself, the client's own; older versions and keys of earlier conventions are swept at boot (`sweepPersistedPayloads`). A held payload of the other unit is never stored; an entry whose shape the client does not read (`isValid`) is ignored. **A change to a saved payload's type needs a version bump**, also when it comes through a shared type (an expense row inside «Últimos movimientos»): `npm run check:conventions` (so the pre-push hook) fingerprints the type each `persistedPayload(…)` call stores (`scripts/check-persisted-payloads.mjs`, recorded in `client/src/queries/persistedPayloadFingerprints.json`) and fails when it changed under the same version; after the bump, `node scripts/check-persisted-payloads.mjs --write`. A change of meaning with the same type (a field switching currency) it cannot see: bump by hand.
- **A background refetch never dims (`queryShowsLoading` in `queries/pageShapeReady.ts`).** A query-backed view's `loading` is `isPending || isPlaceholderData` — no data yet (a disabled query included), or the previous page / filter held — never `isFetching`, because the live-quotes stamp invalidates queries on each price move and everything would dim on every tick. `isLoading` is not the signal either: it is false while placeholder data is shown and for a disabled query. `npm run check:conventions` flags `isFetching` / `isRefetching` in `components/` and `pages/`. **A failed background refetch never hides data either (`queryLoadError`, same file):** react-query keeps the last data and sets `error` when a refetch fails; a view shows its error instead of its content only when it has no data (`queryLoadError(q)` = `q.error` while `q.data` is undefined) — a server restart must not replace a page with an error.
- **Every data component takes `loading?: boolean`** (charts, tables, sections, the CC surfaces, `PeriodReturnsWithBenchmark` with `data: … | null`) and dims its own root. A chart's empty branch is `ChartEmptyState`: while loading an empty `.chart-box--pending` at the chart's size (`boxStyle` for the fixed-height ones), otherwise the «no data» copy — loading and absence never share a message. Tables render their header with no rows while loading; the empty label is for a loaded, empty result.
- **A Diario chart never draws the monthly data.** In day mode a chart shows its pending frame — the title row with its live controls over an empty box of the chart's size (`ChartEmptyState` with `loading`) — until its own daily payload for the current unit has arrived (query neither pending nor placeholder, `queryShowsLoading`); the page passes it no points (an empty block, `null`, `[]`), never `dailyBlock ?? monthlyBlock`. Monthly and yearly modes are unaffected. Applies to the dashboard overview and composition, the group valuation / composition / P/L charts, the Pasivos coverage chart, the card historial and the account valuation and P/L combos. The home net-worth chart shows the same pending frame in Mensual and Anual until the real bundle is in (the placeholder bundle's zero lines are not drawn, since they only flash before the lines' draw-in). A group's P/L combo stays mounted in day mode whatever its points say (`groupPerfAbsent`), so the controls can switch back.
- **Recharts is patched (`patches/recharts+3.10.1.patch`, applied by the root `postinstall: patch-package`).** A Line's first draw-in never recorded reaching the end (its final frame, elapsed 1, renders after `isAnimating` turns false, so `useAnimatedLineLength` is never asked for it, and its 4-decimal rounding can leave even that frame short of `getTotalLength()`), so the first data update redrew the right edge of every line. Changes (both builds, `es6` and `lib`): `Line.js` passes the final frame to `getVisibleLength`; `useAnimatedLineLength.js` counts elapsed 1 as full; `LineDrawShape.js` gives an unmeasured line (length 0 on first render) `stroke-dasharray: 0px 1px` (hidden) instead of `0px 0px`, which the browser draws solid, flashing every line in full before its wipe-in. Still needed in 3.11; drop the patch once Recharts fixes it, and re-check it on any Recharts upgrade (patch-package fails the install when it no longer applies). The deploy re-runs `npm ci` when `patches/` changes (`needs_ci` in `scripts/server-deploy.sh`).
- **Charts change only when what they show changes (`components/charts/stableChartInputs.ts`, used by `AppComposedChart` / `AppLineChart`).** Recharts restarts a series' animation whenever its computed points are a new object, and those are rebuilt from any new `data` array and from any axis whose `tickFormatter` or `ticks` is a new reference (axes compare them by `===`); a page re-rendered by the live-quotes poll therefore re-animated every chart, and an Area hid its labels meanwhile. The wrappers keep three things stable: (1) `data`, structurally shared with the previous one (`useStructuralValue`, `client/src/useStructuralValue.ts`: `replaceEqualDeep`, so equal rows keep their objects and an equal result is the previous array); (2) each `<XAxis>` / `<YAxis>`: equal array and object props stay the previous objects, and `tickFormatter` is one forwarding function to the latest formatter, replaced whenever the formatter's output changes on the axis's ticks, a few of its categories or a set of probe numbers (language, decimal separator, unit, compact format), so labels are never stale; (3) series (`Line`, `Area`, `Bar`, `Scatter`) animate only for 2 s after the chart's shape changes — first draw, row count, first or last category, the set of series, placeholder zeros becoming values, or the wrapper's `animationKey` — so a live update of today's point redraws without a tween. A series still animating when the window ends (one first drawn in a hidden tab, where requestAnimationFrame does not run) remounts once under a new key, since Recharts never ends an animation switched off mid-way and would keep its labels hidden; only that series, because a remounted Line has not measured its path and the next change of shape would wipe it in instead of tweening (the wrapper tracks each series' start and end through one stable callback per series). So pages pass formatters, arrays and colour functions inline without cost, and a derived value a chart or memo reads keeps its identity with `useStructuralValue` (the home «Flujos» points, the persisted home panels, the valuation chart rows). Chart end labels sit on `ReferenceDot`s, not an Area's `label` (`StackedValueAreaChart`), so they never follow a series' animation.
- **Loading is not absence.** A server-stated absence (an explicit `null`, an empty list after load, a capability schema such as `movement_create`) hides the subview; a pending query must not read as one. `period_returns` is `undefined` while loading and `null` when the server says none (`useGroupInfoConsolidatedTables`, `useAccountDetailPageData`); the account placeholder takes its category and title from the cached nav row / nav node (`placeholders/accountDetailPlaceholders.ts`), so category-keyed blocks mount right at first paint; the card strip mounts on a cold cache from `buildPlaceholderNavStripDash` (zero `card_metrics_by_slug` for every nav group node, so `requireNavCardMetrics` holds). Accepted pop-ins, because the client cannot know them before the bundle: the CC/mortgage sections and perf combos of a group that turns out to have none, the capability forms. The dashboard's `chart_shape` and the group pages' `chart_buckets` are the nav-time hints for the patrimonio/perf panels and the grouped toggle.
- **Keep-previous-data is scoped to the entity** (`displayUnitQueryBehaviorFor(queryKey, entityKeyLength)` in `queries/displayUnitQueries.ts`): a unit, period, range, page or filter change holds the previous payload (the page dims it; `isPlaceholderData` means held data of the SAME group/account/scope), while a different group, account or daily scope starts from its placeholder (bare `keepPreviousData` would show account A's bundle, undimmed, under account B's title). **A held prior-unit payload is never fx-converted:** a unit switch shows the target unit's own react-query data when still warm, else the dimmed zero placeholders for charts and tables — and for the CARD STRIP the previous unit's cards, dimmed and in THEIR unit, which flip to the new values once they land (the dashboard from its held bundle; the group, Pasivos and account pages through `useHeldNavStrip`, the previous unit's nav context or stored snapshot, the account hero from its held detail row — `AccountDetailPageData.stripUnit`); the target unit's stored snapshot wins when it has one; a blank strip only when neither unit has anything. The nav snapshot and nav context queries are keyed on the unit (entity length 2) so neither holds one unit's payload under another; the dashboard cards always animate. The ticker, watchlist and Flows P/L keep previous data across their unit/window changes too. Pages gate `notFound` / `loadFailed` / `<Navigate>` on the nav query having settled, never while it is pending.

## UI tables — desktop/mobile sync

Parallel-renderings rule: GUIDELINES.md. A table drawn from one column list uses `ColumnTable` (`components/ui/ColumnTable.tsx`: each `TableColumn` gives the header, the desktop cell and how the card shows it, so the two cannot drift); a hand-paired table keeps the two renderings side by side: desktop columns are `<td className="desktop-only">`; mobile is `<TableMobileCard>` / `<TableMobileCardRow>` inside `<td className="mobile-only">`, usually in the same component or a companion (e.g. `CreditCardSections.tsx` + `CreditCardPurchaseMobileCard.tsx`).

**A table shows its cards whenever its desktop rendering does not fit** (`useTableFit.ts`, `Table` `fitToWidth`): below 880 px, and at any width when the desktop columns exceed the `.table-wrap` — `Table` measures after every render and wrap resize (class removed, measured, reset; synchronous, before paint, no state) and sets `.table-wrap--cards`, styled exactly like the narrow viewport (rule sets side by side). Default for every `table--parallel-mobile` table, so both renderings switch together; a fixed breakpoint can't work because a table is as wide as its data. Rentabilidad (`PeriodReturnsTable.tsx`) is a row list, never a table: a heading row with the «vs <benchmark> ▾» picker, then one line per period carrying a % or a P/L of its own (other periods are left out; a list with none says so in one line), every figure named to a screen reader by a visually-hidden label (the column labels are visual only), a null figure «—» once loaded (blank only while loading). The full list (dashboard, Pasivos group pages, standard account pages) lines up as subgrid columns — %, P/L, IRR, gap bars (your % over the benchmark's, every row from one shared zero — mid-track at most, left when nothing is negative — each on its own scale), the benchmark's %, P/L and IRR, Δ — and below 54rem of its frame (container `period-returns`) each period becomes three lines (%, P/L, Δ; the bars; the IRRs and the benchmark's figures, muted, marked by the bar's swatch); below 26rem Δ moves under the P/L beside the bars. The compact list (`compact`: glance pages, position pages) shows %, P/L and the benchmark's % per period. Never fix overflow with a scroll box — one exception: the watchlist heat grid (`WatchlistPage.tsx`, `pages.css` `watchlist-heat`) keeps its nine return windows on one line and, when its container is under 40rem (a phone), scrolls inside its own `.table-wrap` (positioned, so the visually-hidden header labels stay inside it) under a pinned checkbox and symbol (the symbol capped at 7rem, a fetch error showing its label only); from 40rem up it fits because the symbol wraps and the cells tighten under 52rem (minimum ≈ 584 px at 880, 689 px above 52rem), and the document still never scrolls sideways. A right-edge card's number-flow mask spills ~6 px, so card blocks at the content edge use `overflow-x: clip` (`.cards-strip`, home «Flujos» period cards). Check pages at 375 / 599 / 600 / 879 / 880 / 1024 / 1279 / 1280 / 1439 / 1440 / 1600 / 1920 for the document's scrollWidth = clientWidth, and on iOS Safari (the Simulator) too: Chrome's mobile emulation does not show Safari's CSS gaps. **One container per `@container` rule, never a list** (`@container a (…), b (…)`): Safari drops the whole rule. A list whose breakpoint depends on its kind keeps one container name and states the breakpoint as the frame's `font-size`, queried as `1em` (resolved against the container; `PerfDetailRows.module.css`, `FlowRows.module.css`); the list sets its own font-size.

**Rows that open in place** (`components/ui/OpenRows.tsx`, container `open-rows`): the app messages (`MessageList`) and every panel of `/panel/import-sync` are one row list, one rendering at every width. A row is lead (a date, mono) | title | status word (`StatusWord`: ok green, active blue, bad red, muted) | a muted first line that cuts with «…» | action | ›; with children it is a button that opens them under the line (`RowFacts` for label / value pairs), without it is plain text with no chevron. The action (Run now, Cancel, Retry, Mark stale, Edit / Remove) sits on the line beside the toggle, never inside it, in the same grid row. Wide, the list is the grid and each line a subgrid, so cells line up down the list (a cell a row lacks gives its track to its neighbour: `--title-col`, `--first-col`); under 40rem of its frame a line is title, status, action and chevron, then the lead and the first line (`leadInline`, the messages: the date stays right of the title). The import sync panels (`components/sync/`): runs (latest run's time, kind, outcome, next slot), the queue (running and waiting jobs, then the finished ones the server sends, folded after the server's `recent_shown` behind «Show more…»; the first line is, worded by the client from the server's fields, why a queued job waits, a running job's worker, heartbeat and lease, or a finished one's failed steps or error, else why it was queued), the market sources (state, next sync, the day when it is not a trading day; a publisher's lag reads as waiting), the documents (one line per year from the server's `years` — how many columns have every counted month and, from its `incomplete_columns`, the short ones by name; a column with no document by that year is null and not drawn — opening into that year's months × columns as marks; the server decides each cell's `state`, a month before a column's first document is `before_first` (empty) and the month in progress with no document yet `not_due`, neither counted — a year with only that month reads «nothing due yet»; under 40rem the matrix shares the frame's width and the column names stand upright) and the one-off merchants (a line per name; Edit turns the line into the field).

**Expense line lists** (`CreditCardExpenseLinesTable.tsx`): month, facturación, «sin categoría», big-group and deposit-matched lists are one responsive list (no horizontal scroll, same rendering at every width; a container query moves pills under the merchant when narrow). Header = three sort buttons (date, category, amount). A popover inside a `Modal` renders into its `<dialog>` (`AnchoredPopover`), since `showModal()` puts the dialog in the top layer.

**The flows list** (`FlowRows.tsx`, under `FlowsTable`'s filters): a row list, not a table. When every row fits on one line, rows are subgrids under a header — date, type pill, accounts, amount (units superscript; on an account page whose account holds units, units are a column of their own after the amount), note (the one flexible, ellipsized column); below the container breakpoint (58rem when both accounts are named, 44rem on an account page, 50rem with the units column) a row is three lines: date + type + amount (and units), accounts, note. **An intra-group transfer shows once** (`assembleFlowRows`, `flowsApi.ts`): the origin's row, `internal`, unsigned, with a side-neutral type (`internalTransferFlowType`: «Traspaso <CODE>», or «Cambio de divisas» across currencies; share purchases and card payments keep their type); account/bucket filters match either side (`counterpart_bucket_slug`). A transfer leaving the group keeps its signed leg. Every row carries `from_account_*` / `to_account_*` and `money_direction` (`moneyDirectionForAccount`: by leg for transfers, else by signed amount in its own currency, then units; null only for a zero opening anchor; anything else undecidable throws). Labels naming a currency take it from the movement (`movementFlowTypeLabel(type, {currency, counter_currency})`). Accounts cell (`flowAccountsCell`): on a group/dashboard origin → target («→ account» from outside, «account →» leaving); on an account page the page's account is omitted and the arrow shows left (→) or arrived (←), then the other account.

**The income page's lists** (`components/income/`, styles `IncomeRows.module.css`): row lists, one rendering at every width (container `income-rows`, 46rem: wide rows are subgrids of their list, narrow rows wrap onto two or three lines; a line row's kind slot is capped at 14rem and wraps, its name keeps 8rem, its account shrinks to an ellipsis). `IncomeMonthList`: a row per month (or per year, which opens into its months) with the period's income as a bar by kind on the page's shared scale, each kind's amount (on a phone a line under the bar), its total and its line count; a month opens into its lines — the server's `aggregates.months[].lines` (`FlowIncomeMonthLine`: the record, its kind and its amount, newest first, a salary on its payroll month), which the client only looks up in `lines` / `manual` / `work_earnings` — each a checking credit with its kind as a `PillPicker` (`IncomeKindPicker`: «unclassified» is the unset pill; rent per place; «refund of spending» moves it out of income) and «Exclude», a salary with its payslip's gross − deductions and a «receipt» link. There is no separate list of every income line. `PayslipList`: a row per payslip (period, employer, gross as a bar split into net pay and each deduction group, net pay, the legal-parameter check) opening into its receipt — printed earnings, the breakdown's deductions, net pay, the linked credit, the type, the check differences in full; the printed earnings lines are the liquidación's pesos (`formatPayslipPesos`) whatever the unit. «Año» lists each year's payslips added up (net pay split salary | severance by the server's `net_severance`), opening into its deductions with their share of gross, net pay as a neutral total with its salary and severance parts, and the employer's 401(k) match. Net pay is drawn in its income kind's colour here and in the «Bruto vs líquido» chart (a `net_severance` series beside `net`; severance amber, so no deduction is amber or orange). A receipt's printed lines end on the printed gross in pesos, with the gross in the unit under it when the unit is not pesos. Each opening row names its figures in `aria-label`; the «receipt» link moves focus to the payslip. It replaces the gross/deductions table and the payslip table (`payroll_breakdown.payslips` covers every `work_earnings` row; a payslip without one throws). `IncomeSideLists`: refunds, auto-filtered and excluded credits as collapsed sections whose heading carries the line count and the server's sum (`list_totals`: `amount` and `unpriced`, the lines with no rate on file it leaves out, said in the heading); an excluded line's note is a line of its own under the row. Counts pick a `…One` key at 1 (the app has no plural suffixes). Kind and payroll colours are one list each (`incomeColors.ts`), shared with the two charts.

**The deposits, P/L and real-estate lists** (`components/flows/DepositMonthList.tsx`, `FlowsPlAccountRows.tsx`, `components/real-estate/RealEstateBillMonthList.tsx`, styles `components/flows/FlowMonthRows.module.css`, container `flow-rows`): row lists, one rendering at every width.
