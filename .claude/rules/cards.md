## CC chart / Pasivos convention

**"owed on that date", not billing-month totals.** Per-card chart lines, the Pasivos Total, `accountMarkClpAtYmd` and the dashboard Pasivos line read stored `valuations` via `latestCreditCardValuationRowAsOf`. **Every date, today included, is walked:** `last anchor strictly before it + evidence dated after it` (`ccOwedWalkClpAtYmd` in `ccOwedWalk.ts`, the choke point `latestCreditCardDisplayedBalance` uses; `as_of_date` returns the walked-through date so callers don't re-apply the window). Never value today with the live billing formula (facturado + cupo en cuotas − cuota a pagar next mes): it is a different framing of the same debt and dumps the frame gap into today's delta. Today's stamp is written from the walk, so tomorrow's anchor shares its frame. Billing surfaces (Balance total, detalle por mes, cupo) keep the live formula. No separate "Saldo pasivos" line (Total carries it). Month-end points written by `upsertCreditCardValuationsFromLedger` = billing-detail `balance_total` = **facturado + post-close plan remainder** (`installmentRemainingClpByCalendarMonth`, cuotas due strictly after the month) + post-cierre activity (charges +, PAGOs −), re-synced by the card-line write that changed them (`cards/lineWrites.ts`). Don't subtract «cuota a pagar next mes» from an anchor: that only nets a double count in the bank's live cupo frame and sinks closed anchors one cuota low. Re-stamp: `server/scripts/restamp-cc-anchor-frame.ts`. **Today-stamps self-purge on contradicted evidence:** every CC write also stamps `today`, valid only until dated evidence for an earlier day arrives, so the writer takes **`affectedEvidenceFromYmd`** and deletes every non-month-end stamp from that date through yesterday before rewriting (the purge runs before the has-points early return). **Every caller that adds or removes dated evidence passes it**: the merge import (`earliestInsertedTxDate` over genuinely inserted lines — dedupe skips contribute nothing, so re-pasting is idempotent — plus new `purchase_date`s and the header pagado date), line deletions (`earliestTransactionDateForLineIds`, read BEFORE deleting), manual installment create/edit/delete (edits use min(old, new)). Pure recomputes omit it.

- **A card's P/L is its cost of financing, loss-negative.** Liabilities mark as positive debt; every P/L surface uses `pl = prior − owed − flow`. Card P/L = **−(section-3 charges: intereses, comisiones, impuestos)** on the day charged (`ccFinancingCostDaily.ts`; a financing charge is the line's stored `kind` `financing_charge`, set at write by the contracts' `cards/lineRules.ts`; held by `ccFinancingIdentity.test.ts`). Everything else is **flow, derived** (`flow = −Δowed + financing`), never summed from events, because cierre re-splits of the anchors are measurement noise; ordinary days read `pl = 0`. **Installment interest** (`ccInstallmentInterest.ts`): for a plan printed with rate > 0, interest = Σ printed cuotas − printed principal, dated on the purchase date (the owed walk adds the whole total then, so flow stays at the principal); a 0% plan rounding a few pesos over its principal, or a plan no statement has printed yet, has none. The dashboard's financing summary reads it in the purchase's billing month. **In «deuda en cuotas» interest bills cuota by cuota** (`ccInstallmentInterestBillingEvents`): principal on the purchase date, each cuota's interest share at the close that bills it (projected close if unbilled); retirement removes whole cuotas. The owed walk and P/L keep the whole total on the purchase date. **The fx move on unpaid foreign debt is P/L** (`ccRevaluationCostByDate`, see «Card dollars in pesos»). Card flows are not personal capital: out of `/flows/deposits`, the deposits reconciliation and the "aportes acum." companions; the `pago_tarjeta` mirror's card leg emits nothing (`accountDeposits.ts`; the cash leg emits its withdrawal). The mortgage's daily flow leg is `mortgageSheetPaymentEventsThroughDate` (depto ledger).
- **The installment schedule is facturación/statement months everywhere.** `purchaseFirstDueYm` + index = the month each cuota **bills**; plan sums per month equal the statements' billed cuotas; `cuotaAPagarNextMesClp` / facturaciones read the billing month itself (never +1). First-due precedence: cuota-01 statement month → cuota-00 preamble's statement month + 1 → stored `first_due_month` (web-paste nudge, write-once) → manual guess = the purchase's own cycle (`billingMonthContainingPurchase`). **Manual anchors with no statement cuota evidence clamp to `last fully-closed month + 1`** (an imported statement that didn't bill the plan is negative evidence; statement-driven, never calendar). **A facturación closes only when every statement currency the card's history carries is imported** (`lastPdfBillingMonthForAccount` / `requiredStatementCurrenciesForAccount`, `cards/facturacion.ts`): a lone USD twin must not advance `open_billing_month` or retarget pastes. The statement's bucket settlement settles the bucket per currency as each twin arrives (keeping the whole bucket would double-count). A dormant never-billed USD side closes on CLP alone, and a USD twin still missing once the bank has closed a LATER facturación (later statement, feed-observed close, or passed announced close) is not coming — the month closes on CLP alone (`pdfClosedBillingMonthsForAccount`); the bucket reconcile keeps the stricter `hasEveryStatementTwinForBillingMonth`, so a CLP-only month's dollar lines wait for a USD statement. A card that stopped billing (`cardStoppedBilling`, `cards/lifecycle.ts`) gets no provisional rows. Web-paste plans carry the issuer card_group (`'santander'`/`'BCI'`), PDF ones the parser groups (`'A'`/`'B'`/`'INTL'`); the manual↔statement twin reconcile treats the web-paste group as compatible with any PDF group and tolerates ±2 days on indexed cuota lines (exact merchant/amount/cuotas required). Nota-cancelled plans (full-principal NOTA DE CREDITO matched by posting **statement month** — banks backdate transaction dates — or a strictly-later transaction date) are excluded from every schedule aggregate via `schedulePurchases`. A settled ledger doesn't force-active a card (`cardStoppedBilling` needs outstanding principal, else the tail heuristic).
- **Cuotas por facturación (`payload.months`)**: one row per facturación — `month`, `pay_by_date` (printed PAGAR HASTA when closed, derived for open/projected), `total`, `debt_after` (suffix sum; last 0; pesos, the payload's `billing_currency`). Rows roll off once the pay-by passes (Chile day); `totals.next_calendar_month` = the **pay** month of the next payment. Purchase tables show `purchase_date` + `purchase_billing_month` (`billingMonthContainingPurchase`).
- **Facturación proxy earnings are keyed by the facturación that billed the money** (`ccInvestmentProxy.ts`, column `facturacion_proxy` by `billing_month`): each purchase floats from its date to that facturación's pay-by. A printed cuota is keyed by `first_due_month` + N − 1 and withdrawn on its payment row's `pay_by_date`; a one-shot line by `facturacionMonthByStatementDate` (stale bucket → open month) and withdrawn on that pay-by (`facturacionPayByIsoResolver`), so open/provisional paste lines count. Superseded lines and count-less cuota purchases are out; an unparseable line date throws.
- **Months after the open facturación are plan-only, pay-frame projections** (`projected: true`, `appendProjectedBillingDetailRows`): facturado null; saldo/cupo at month-end M = plan remainder after (M−1)'s close (cuotas leave the debt on their pay-by, not their close). One step per month, flat from today through the open month-end, plus a trailing zero month. Identity: chart month-end M = calendar row M's cuota + `debt_after`. Closed months keep the statement frame; the seam is smooth because bank-frame(M) ≡ pay-frame(M+1). `latestBillingDetailRow` skips projected rows. **The historial chart's two lines are the daily walks sampled** (`ccHistorialLinesAtMonthEndsClp` → `linesForMonths` of `buildCcHistorialChartSeries`, from the card payload and `creditCardGroupLedgerResponse` over the daily block's masters): «saldo total» (owed walk) and «deuda en cuotas» (plan-debt walk) at each month's last day, today for the current month, the plan tail after; bars per facturación. The detalle's open-month roll-forward adds plans bought after the closed month's calendar month-end (`installmentPurchasesClpAfter`); its «cupo en cuotas» keeps the statement frame (facturado + cupo = balance).
- **Historial bars: one stacked bar per facturación** (`buildCcHistorialChartSeries`), bottom to top «Facturado CLP» (CLP facturado − cuotas, net of notas, below zero when negative via `stackOffset="sign"`), «Facturado CLP (cuotas)» (`facturado_cuotas`, the facturación's `cuota_a_pagar`) and the foreign segment (`facturado_foreign_total`, pesos at the paid rate; tooltip each currency's amount and «($Y)»), one amber hue, cuotas darkest; tooltip top to bottom (saldo total, deuda en cuotas, facturado over segments). A projection-only month carries just its cuotas; an open month with no lines yet splits its detail facturado as CLP. **A group's bar is Σ its cards' bars** (`memberSeries`), and the merged billing-detail row adds a projected member's cuota to a month another member billed (`mergedTotalFacturadoClp`, «≈ cuota»); never re-split bars from merged facturaciones (they miss the projected member's cuotas). **Day mode: one stack per billing month** (`facturacionBarsOnMonthCloseDates`, same cached bundle): Σ of members' bars, dated on the latest close among members that billed it (`closeEvidenceForBillingMonth` for projected); a member with no bar lends neither pesos nor close. The client extends the grid past the plan tail to a later close, draws fixed 6 px segments (Recharts shrinks `barSize` to sub-pixel day bands) and lists bars within `athTooltipIndexTolerance` rows. No «Coste financiero vs facturado» chart, no `extraOffsets`; stale `nw-credit-card-extra-offsets:<id>` keys are swept at boot (`DisplayPreferencesContext`).

- **Card foreign debt: values follow the date's rate, costs keep the paid rate** (`ccOwedWalk.ts`, migration 259, `docs/holdings-cards-plan.md`). A card owes a sub-balance per currency it bills in (`CcOwedNative`, map by currency, pesos always; test issuer `euronova`, `ccEuroCardOwed.test.ts`); its value on a date (owed walk, anchors, Pasivos, cash netting, detalle month frame, daily CC block) converts each at that date's rate with `convertCurrency` (debt side; a currency in credit at the asset side; live today). Evidence stays in the bank's currency (`normalizedPostCloseLines` carry `currency` + `amount`; a foreign line's statement-frame pesos at the stored mid on its facturación's debt-frame date, `debtCardLineAmountClp` — one path for every currency, and a sum throws on a foreign amount with no rate). **Statement lines store `amount` + `currency` + `cuota_amount`** (migration 319; a line's currency is its own — an open bucket is one peso statement holding dollar lines; no `amount_clp`/`amount_usd`/`valor_cuota_mensual_*` columns, dropped by 324). The peso/dollar view is `cards/lineAmountSql.ts` (`lineAmountClpSql` / `lineAmountUsdSql` / `lineCuotaClpSql` / `lineCuotaUsdSql`, `lineLegacyAmounts`: peso line = pesos + NULL dollars; dollar line = 0 pesos + dollars; other currency NULL in all four); kind `cc_line_kind_native` (`cardLineKind276.ts`); the import fingerprint hashes the legacy shape; `legacyAmountColumn` (`cards/statementStorage.ts`) is the one mapping. Anchors are per currency in `cc_owed_anchors`; month-end anchors split the statement frame by currency via `ccStatementOwedByCurrencyByMonth`, computed for every foreign currency the card bills (`cardForeignCurrencies`, `ccFacturacionForeignRate.ts`) at uniform rate 1000 and per currency at 900/1100 (stand-in `DebtFrameRates` from `debtFrameRates`, a map by currency passed down to `debtFrameClpPerUnit` in `ccExpenseAmountClp.ts`), throwing unless affine in each rate; `valuations` keeps one CLP row per anchor date at that date's rate (group charts, liabilities overview). A traspaso de deuda moves foreign out and pesos in on the same day. **Fx move is P/L (user decision):** `ccFinancingCostByDateInUnit(card, unit)` (`ccFinancingCostDaily.ts`) = section-3 charges + plan interest (pesos: `ccSection3FinancingClpByDate`; other units: each charge at its own day's debt-side rate) + `ccRevaluationCostByDate(card, unit)` (each sub-balance in another currency than the unit × the day's rate change: in pesos, foreign owed at previous day-end × Δ buy rate; in dollars, pesos owed × Δ sell rate). **A foreign payment counts at the pesos actually paid (user decision)** where its real rate is on file (`foreignDebtPaymentsForAccount(card, currency)`: paired divisas purchase, a traspaso's peso leg, the traced cost from a foreign cash account, `foreignCashCostLots.ts`): the payment day adds `pesos paid − foreign paid × day rate`, so its fx P/L is `foreign × (real rate − previous day's debt rate)` and its flow the pesos paid; a traspaso's step against the buy rate is fx P/L with zero net flow. No real rate on file → the day's rate. Derived flow `−Δowed + cost` in the unit (daily `ccDerivedFlows`; monthly `ccFinancingCostBetweenInUnit`; dashboard day cell), P/L = −cost = Δvalue − flow, days sum to months. For a card in USD read `ccOwedByCurrencyAtYmd`. The statement frame (`balance_statement`: Cupo card's used figure, stored snapshot) converts at the stored mid on pay-by − 1 (`balanceUsdFxDateIso`, `debtFrameClpPerUnit`), never a live rate. Re-stamp: `server/scripts/restamp-cc-owed-anchors.ts [--apply]`. **What a facturación shows it cost** (row's foreign pesos, historial bars, its expense lines) is the paid rate: the payments after its close up to the next close, weighted — divisas purchases (`pago_tarjeta` with a counter leg in that currency), payments from a cash account in that currency and traspasos (`cc_traspaso_deuda_links`); today's mid (live where the pair has a live rate) while unpaid and the pay-by (inclusive) is ahead; the pay-by − 1 stored mid once the pay-by passed with no payment on file. **Per foreign currency** (`ccFacturacionForeignRate.ts`): rows carry `facturado_foreign`, one entry per billed currency (`{currency, amount, billing_amount, rate, rate_source}`; `amount` null on a closed month with no statement, 0 on an open cycle that billed none; a third currency's closed statement bills its header, else its lines, `foreignBilledFromStatementLines`); pay-by and live rates are mids from `rates.ts`, one path for every currency. `rate_source` is `paid` | `live` | `pay_by` (a merged group row names one only when members agree); expense lines read the same cached rows (`facturacionForeignRateWithSourceResolver(card, currency, rows)`) so the modal adds up to the row; the historial foreign segment is `facturado_foreign_total` (label «Facturado en moneda extranjera»). Lines are read in their own currency (`cardLineNativeAmount` in `ccExpenseAmountClp.ts`). A foreign payment line (`isCcForeignPaymentLine`: ABONO DE DIVISAS, PAGO / MONTO CANCELADO in that currency, a traspaso's foreign leg) shows the pesos actually paid (`foreignPaymentLineClpResolver(card, currency, …)`): its paired divisas purchase (via the payment evidence key, see «Checking↔CC payment mirrors»), a traspaso's CLP leg, else the rate of the facturación whose (close, next close] window holds it (`facturacionPaidOn`). The row = the modal's Gastos + Coste financiero; Abonos pay the previous facturación. **Coste financiero is the server's call** (`markFinancingCostLines` in `flowsExpenses.ts`: `is_financing_cost` on positive one-shot section-3 lines per `ccStatementLineRules.json`); never keep a client copy of the rules. Import dedupe keeps the statement-date fx (compares amounts only).
- **A card payment in a foreign currency counts at the pesos that currency cost** (`foreignCashCostLots.ts`, migration 231). A `pago_tarjeta` from a foreign cash account (in that account's currency, no counter leg) enters the paid rate (`foreignDebtPaymentsForAccount(card, currency)`) at a traced cost (`foreignOutflowPesoCostByMovement`, per currency; a conversion from another foreign currency carries its slices' pesos; `market` lots at the stored mid via `rates.ts`, dollars at the `fx_daily` close): lots walked FIFO through the cash accounts in scope (the source plus, recursively, every same-currency cash account that funded one in scope) — `purchase` (a CLP→USD transfer's pesos), `own_transfer` (pesos the source outflow consumed), `market` (other inflows at the stored mid on or before their day; no rate throws). A shortfall above a cent throws, as does a foreign payment that does not leave a cash account in its currency or one the tracer didn't price. **Broker withdrawals reserve at request time:** each `broker_withdrawal_requests` row takes its gross FIFO at `requested_at`; its booked transfer (`incoming_wire_bookings`) carries all reserved pesos for the net, the fee draws the rest at 0; a booked reservation must end empty and a booking whose transfer was deleted throws. Order: Chile date, then time when all are timed, else arrivals first then id (a buy and its funding wire may be written in either order), requests among their account's events — a request's day must have times on every other event of its account, else throw. Times live in **`movement_event_times`** (migration 231: movement → ISO UTC `occurred_at`, `source = 'broker_mail'`, message id), stamped by the Fintual branch of `broker.notifications` for every row it writes, promotes or matches (so re-sending staged mails stamps existing movements); never overwritten (`event_times_stamped` in details).

- **Facturado financing links (`cc_facturado_financing_links`, «Financiamiento de facturado en cuotas»).** A facturado of one card paid by installment purchases on another is a link; `applyCcFacturadoFinancingProjection` (`ccFacturadoFinancingProjectionLines.ts`, last step of the expenses payload) hides the financing cuotas (`excluded`), keeps financed purchases in their month in «Total» mode and in «Por cuota» splits each equally over the payment's distinct cuota billing months as `split_only` lines dated the 28th with `installment_total_clp` (for the «/$total» superscript). **Financing cuotas match by exact `purchase_key`**, so statement cuota lines must carry the same with-total `installment-h:` key as their plan-derived siblings: `installmentLedgerTotalForStatementLine` (`ccExpenseCategories.ts`, behind both the flows `installment_total_clp` stamp and `resolveCcExpensePurchaseKey`) resolves via the line's `cc_installment_payments.parser_row_id`, then identity when matching plans share one total (twins), then the printed cuota among same-identity plans, else the legacy no-total key (still resolved by category/notes readers). Identity-only lookup breaks on twins with different totals. **Interest gap = Σ cuotas − the closed statement's `monto_facturado`** (`statementFacturadoClpForBillingMonth`: CLP, non-web-paste, by statement month; identical duplicate copies collapse, disagreeing totals throw; no closed statement → no gap line; ≤1 peso per cuota is rounding) — never Σ cuotas − Σ financed lines (that reads the financed card's own cuotas and notas as interest). A facturado paid as a one-shot needs no link: `no_cuenta` keeps it out of gastos.

- **Financing links declare themselves** (`ccFacturadoFinancingAuto.ts`, migration 297 `cc_facturado_payment_charges`). After every card write (a listing or statement import, feed import end, a plan created by hand or from a tagged line) one matcher looks for an unlinked closed CLP facturado paid from another card master. Pool: every peso purchase there — cuota plans (at principal: printed for interest plans, else total), feed lines tagged as cuota purchases awaiting a plan, one-shots (`card_purchase`, no installment flag) — each once by flow, dated after the close and up to 10 days past the pay-by, and only where the merchant opens like a place the paid card's issuer is paid at (`facturado_paid_at_merchant_prefixes` on `CC_ISSUERS`: BCI Lider «RECAUDACION», «EXPRESS», «EXP LIDER», «HIP LIDER», «HIPER LIDER», «LIDER»; none for Santander and CMR, so theirs aren't matched). Per paying card and day, combinations summing to the facturado to the peso; **fewest payments win**; interchangeable twins (same card, day, kinds, merchants, amounts) take the lowest ids; any other tie, a purchase fitting two facturados, or a day with >10 candidates writes nothing. Existing links are never touched. **A match is recorded once** — the link row, plans as `finances_facturado` flow links (`upsertCcFacturadoFinancingLink`), one-shots as `cc_facturado_payment_charges` by flow (flow merges move them) — and never re-matched; a plan-less record whose charges no longer hold a one-shot line is dropped and the month re-matched. A match with a tagged line waits for its plan before recording; its PAGO does not wait. Each one-shot charge is filed as an internal transfer («Único» row) unless the line already has an «Único» row, a per-line override, a declined automatic category, or a general rule giving «no cuenta» / internal transfer. **PAGO planting:** if the paid card shows no payment of that amount (payment lines −3…+5 days, or a statement header naming it), a PAGO is planted in its open bucket (batch kind `cc_financing_payment`, registered in `cc_planted_payment_lines`, so the bank's own PAGO in the Lider listing replaces it), and the real statement PAGO settles it. **Partly financed facturado** (charges + plans): only the financed share (facturado − Σ charges) ÷ facturado of each financed purchase spreads over the cuota months, the rest stays in its month (`financing-proj-rest:` split_only lines; a mortgage line's carrying and amortization split likewise); interest = Σ cuotas − financed principal. A hand-made link replaces the record's charges; the manager lists only records with plans. Near misses (recent facturado, no payment, no match, one day's issuer-place charges ≥ half of it) are reported in the plan, never written.
- **Facturación closes come from the bank's evidence, not the config cycle** (migration 185, `ccBillingCloses.ts`). **Announced:** statements print the next cycle (Santander «PRÓXIMO PERÍODO DE FACTURACIÓN …», BCI «Próximo Período de Facturación …») → `cc_statements.next_period_from` / `next_period_to` (`pdf_next_period_*` in `cc_statement_reconcile.py`); the Santander JSON's FechaProxFact fills `next_period_to` (for a PDF-owned close the JSON importer fills it when missing and reports disagreement). Period end vs printed next start gives the issuer's **close-day rule** (`closeDayOffsetDays`): Santander starts the next cycle ON the close day (a close-day purchase bills next month), BCI the day after. **Observed:** the Santander unbilled feed opens with a per-currency **SALDO INICIAL** row dated at the latest close, valued at that facturación's «Monto total facturado»; the scraper keeps it (`saldoInicial` per slide) and `import:santander-movements` records it in `cc_feed_billing_closes` (a repeat changing date or amount throws). Precedence: statement → feed → announced → estimate (`closeEvidenceForBillingMonth`); **the estimate carries the account's latest known close day into the month** (clamped); the config cycle estimates only for a card with no close on record. Never «fix» the config cycle to the real close day: it is the open buckets' `statement_date` identity (`statementCloseDdMmYyyyForBillingMonth`) and must not collide with an incoming statement's date. **The open month advances on the bank's close** (`lastClosedBillingMonthForAccount` = latest statement close, a later feed close, or a later announced close once its close day is over — the close day is still the closing month's open facturación, though a Santander purchase on it bills the next); on that close day the next month is the **next cycle** (`is_next_cycle` on a facturación row with lines, neither open nor closed: its purchases are a segment of their own in the glance, outside the open bracket, and «Próximos pagos» reads the closing month as open), and whatever is entered by hand files by date (`billingMonthForPurchaseFromOpen`: the purchase's own cycle, never before the open month — hand paste, manual-plan projection, the bucket repair pass); months between the last statement and that close are **provisionally closed** (`provisionallyClosedBillingMonthsForAccount`): facturado = feed SALDO INICIAL per currency (a foreign one at the pay-by frame) when observed, else the app's estimate; the billing-detail row is statement-framed (`as_of_kind: statement`, as_of = the real close, `provisional: true`); facturaciones carry `is_provisional_close`, `close_date_source` and, with the bank's figure, `provisional_estimate_total`. The client marks a provisional month with a bare asterisk (same as projected; the hover hint differs), an estimated close «≈», an unprinted pay-by «≈» (`pay_by_source`: `statement` | `derived` | null). **The open month's cupo subtracts what a provisional close billed** (`provisionalBilledCuotasClp` in `cupoEnCuotasForBillingMonth`), since the live cupo marks cuotas billed only once a statement prints them. A provisional close is **not negative schedule evidence**: the manual-plan clamp reads `lastPdfBillingMonthForAccount`. **Open buckets keep their config `statement_date`** (`cc_statements` is unique on source + date and sums group by statement date); displayed closes come from `closeDateForBillingMonth`. **Feed routing:** a post-close feed row goes to the later of the month after the observed close (`rows_billing_month`) and its own cycle (`feedLineBillingMonths` in `cardUnbilledMovementsApply.ts` via `billingMonthContainingPurchase`; one bucket per month through `lineBillingMonths` on `listingStatements`, `ccWebPasteParse.ts`; hand paste unchanged) — needed because the bank rolls its list the night after the close day. Bucket lines under earlier months whose one-shot key the feed still lists move forward (`moveOpenBucketLinesByDedupeKey`, once per target month). The repair pass moves lines from the next cycle's first day (`nextPeriodStartIsoForBillingMonth`) and creates the open bucket only when a line moves in (an eager create leaves retired cards an empty open facturación each month). **The statement settles its bucket by matching, never by a date window** (`planBucketSettlement`, run by the statement-line plan): a bucket line on the statement (same merchant, amount, date ±1 day, `webPasteLineMatchesStatementLine`) is restated in place by that statement line; an unmatched line inside the billed cycle (up to, excluding, the next cycle's first day) is restated by the first unused statement line of the same currency within ±4 days with a matching merchant or the same amount (a differently-settled pre-auth), and otherwise kept as an annulled purchase (see «Annulled card purchases»); an unmatched line outside moves to the open facturación; a later bucket's line is restated only when the statement bills it. An annulled purchase the statement bills after all: the billed line takes the annulment's flow (or the annulment's flow folds into a line on file that is the earlier arrival) and the annulment goes; one it refunds with a real credit keeps the purchase and drops its synthetic refund (`bank_credit`, annulled on the credit's day). Before the month is fully closed only the arrived twin's currency settles. **Stale buckets** carried into the open month are only those of statement-closed months. **One rule assigns a statement's lines to a facturación:** `facturacionMonthByStatementDate` (own month; a stale bucket's → open month); open-month facturado/payment sums use it via `statementDatesForFacturacion`; expense lines' `billing_month` is stamped from it with a `web_paste` flag for lines the modal may delete; the facturación modal is a plain filter (`flowLinesForFacturacionMonth`). Never keep a client copy of the rule. **Cross-check:** a statement for a feed-observed close must print the same date and bill exactly the SALDO INICIAL (CLP to the peso, USD to the cent) — `assertFeedClosesMatchStatements` throws in every card merge and feed import. A pasted «SALDO INICIAL» row is skipped (`skipped_saldo_inicial`).

- **A derived pay-by follows the issuer** (`CcDerivedPayByRule` on `CC_ISSUERS`, `derivedPayByIso` in `ccInstallmentPayBy.ts`, `ccDerivedPayByRuleForAccount` in `ccIssuerForAccount.ts`). No document announces the next PAGAR HASTA (the Santander JSON's `FechaVenc` is the current statement's own), so open, provisional and projected pay-bys derive from the close: **BCI** the 10th of the next month as it falls, weekends included; **Santander** the 10th moved back to the previous Chile business day (sometimes a day late vs print, never early); **CMR** close + 16 days. Issuer = `credit_card_account_config.issuer` (`ccIssuerSlugForMasterAccount`; no config row or an unlisted issuer throws). - **A statement keeps the rows on file: plan, then restate** (`cards/statementLinePlan.ts`, `cards/statementLinePlanApply.ts`; `importStatementDocuments` — `replaceStatementLines`, the statement JSON write, the PDF upload, the parsed corpus). Before anything is written the planner says which row each document line ends on, through the import's own matchers in the import's order: a row on file the statement bills — its open-bucket line (the bucket settlement below), the line of its earlier copy on a re-import (same parser row, then identity: account, currency, date, amounts, cuota numbers, merchant letters and digits; twins in id order; a statement's cuota line by its parser row), the JSON close a PDF takes over — is **restated in place**: it keeps its id, its flow (category «Único» row, big group, note, pieces, bill link), its arrival stamp and event time, and takes the document's statement, wording, day and amount (`restateCardLineRow`, `cards/lineRowWrites.ts`), the listing's evidence staying on the flow as history. Several rows of one charge (pre-authorisations billed as one): the earliest seen is kept, the others fold into it (`foldCardLineFlowInto`): the earliest-seen row's assignments win (`compareArrival`; ties: the kept row's), whichever row physically stays. A settlement by a statement line already on file (its twin's statement arrived first, a late listing of the purchase sits in the bucket) is a fold too: the bucket line — or the annulled purchase that line billed — folds into it, never deleted with its assignments; the earlier arrival's assignments win. A source takeover of one close (a JSON close its PDF takes over, a statement re-sent) pairs what the exact pairing leaves, only within that close (close day and currency), one to one — the same merchant for every line first, then a looser one — by the bucket's settle figures (currency, amount to the coin, day within a day) and a merchant that matches or differs only in rendering (`merchantRenderingKey`, the JSON↔PDF cross-check's: a terminal code, punctuation), so a JSON line printed «NAME 1234» is the PDF's «NAME», restated. A document line no row is gets a new row, stamped as an arrival unless it is a statement's cuota line; a new line beside kept lines of its identity copies their category and big group. Gates run as before, inside the same card-line write (the reconcile assert, feed closes, one statement per facturación, the plan ledger asserted to remove exactly the one-shot lines the plan says), and a gate failure writes nothing. **A document already on file as it states writes nothing** — every header the same (`resolveCcStatementHeader`), every kept row already stating its line, nothing to insert, fold, remove or settle (`statementPlanWritesNothing`): no row, no change-log entry, no session finish. **Ambiguity:** a re-import identity group whose counts differ keeps its defined outcome (the vanished lines removed, their flows retired with what was on them, reported as `unpaired`; the document lines written); anything else the plan cannot pair one to one (two document lines ending on one row) fails the import of that document, naming the rows, before anything is written. Listings (paste, feed, planted payment) never replace a row: the listing merge (`mergeListingStatements`, `importCcStatementsMerge`) writes the lines no row on file repeats (dedupe key, a plan's purchase, a fuzzy duplicate, a planted charge's later listing) and refuses a statement; when one of its passes removes a line for the line that replaces it under another merchant, the plan carries it (`CardLinePlan.carry` → `adoptReplacingCardLines`: a cut/pending re-listing dropped for its fuller twin, `planTruncatedMerchantDuplicateRemoval`; a planted payment replaced by the bank's line). Two things stay carry rules, not flow state: per-line category overrides (keyed by line id; `snapshotLineCategories` / `restoreLineCategories`) and merchant rules — when no rule reaches the replacing line's name, the rule is added under it (the statement prints merchants differently, e.g. BCI's « (T)»). `scripts/report-document-import.ts --db=<snapshot copy> --account= --statements=|--cartola=` prints a statement's plan and what the production import would write (rows restated, inserted, folded, removed; stamps; the session finish), in a rolled-back transaction; `--db=<copy> --preflight` (read only, before migrating) lists what migrations 371–373 would trip on — every list empty = safe to deploy. There is no statement wipe: `import:cc-parsed --full` re-plans every statement over the rows on file. **Merchant rules match the statement's name too** (`vendorRuleKeyCandidates` in `bankMerchantName.ts`, most specific first): its vendor key — the bank's clutter such as the PDF's glued « COMPRAS P.A.T.» dropped —, then the key of its 15-character cut. The history repair `scripts/archive/repair-cc-expense-orphaned-line-keys.ts` is archived (it imports the deleted `ccExpenseLineRekey`).

## Open-month CC bucket — truncated merchant duplicates

- The open month gets **two renderings of one transaction**: the nightly feed (`card.unbilled_movements` → `insertLines` in `cards/lineWrites.ts`, `cc_santander_fetch`) with the full merchant on settled rows, and a manual web paste — or the feed's own pending rows — cut at **exactly 15 chars**. The one-shot key `merchant | amount | date` won't collide (and dates often shift a day), so both would survive.
- **`planTruncatedMerchantDuplicateRemoval`** (`ccTruncatedMerchantDedupe.ts`) runs after every paste/feed write and drops the shorter one once the fuller twin exists: scoped to `import:web-paste%`, same amount to the cent and currency, **identical merchants never match** (real repeat spending; the key handles true dups), fires only with both present. (1) **Truncated** (`isTruncatedMerchantOf`): shorter exactly 15 chars and a strict prefix, dates within `MATCH_WINDOWS.truncated_merchant`. (2) **Pending → settled with terminal code** (`isPendingAuthorizationOf`): shorter < 15 chars, longer ends in a spaced 4-digit code (` NNNN`) whose core is the shorter name or it plus more words, SAME date (settling keeps the date). Glued codes (25-char overflow) imply a 15-char cut → rule 1. Idempotent; planner `planTruncatedMerchantDuplicateLines`, script `server/scripts/remove-cc-merchant-duplicate-lines.ts` (report-first; `--apply` = evidence-date read-before-delete + revaluation + billing recompute).
- **The bank's feed can itself list one charge twice** (different `Ciudad` formatting, e.g. `APPLE.COM BILL` / `APPLE.COM/BILL`): bank data, re-inserted if deleted; it resolves at the facturación, when the statement settles the bucket (the billed row restated, the extra one folded or annulled). Same for pending rows restated with an IDENTICAL merchant a day later — deliberately not deduped.
- **Pending international authorizations** in the feed have `DescripcionRubro: null` / `TipoBen: null`, `Comercio` cut at 15; the settled row (same `Fecha`, `Importe`, full name + code) replaces them a day or two later; rules 1–2 remove the pending twin.
- **Cuota-billing reference rows never import.** At a close the feed/web table re-lists billed cuotas as `CUOT: <cuota №>OPER: <plan №>` at the cuota amount; the schedule already bills them (billing never changes owed) and the overlap skip can't match them. `listingStatements` routes any `isCcCuotaBillingReferenceMerchant` line (incl. the 15-char cut) to a reported `skipped_cuota_billing` bucket (batch log + import panel, never silent), unconditionally (plan-gating would let a not-yet-converted plan's cuota-1 through). Old rows: `server/scripts/remove-cc-cuota-billing-lines.ts` (report-first, `--apply`).

Built once from mailed statements (`pdftotext -layout` into `cfraser/cmr/text/`; each must reproduce its printed billed total from the previous plus lines). CMR FALABELLA» debits are `pago_tarjeta` (`CC_PAYMENT_DESC_RE`); CMR → MACH top-up is `carga_tarjeta`.
- **Issuer data:** each `CC_ISSUERS` row has `statement_currencies: {primary, secondary[]}` (Chilean three: `clp` + `["usd"]` when active), `installments`, `unbilled_listing`. Close rule `closedBillingMonthsFromStatements` (`cards/facturacion.ts`, pure): primary statement in, and each required secondary (`requiredStatementCurrenciesForAccount` = primary ∪ secondaries the stream carries) in or proven not coming by the later-close rule; slot = `Map<currency, statement>`; `hasEveryStatementTwinForBillingMonth` takes the set. **One statement per currency per facturación:** two different closes of one currency in one billing month (calendar month of `period_to`, e.g. the 1st and the 31st) throw (`assertOneStatementPerFacturacion` / `sameMonthStatementConflicts`, `cards/facturacion.ts`) — inside every card write's transaction (the listing merge, the statement import) and in `closedBillingMonthsFromStatements` on read — never merge into one row; rows sharing one close are copies of one statement, not a conflict. ## Refunds inside gastos; additional-card reimbursements (migration 217, `checkingExpenseRefunds.ts`, `additionalCardReimbursements.ts`)

- **A checking credit can be a refund of spending** (`checking_expense_refunds`: movement → category): listed apart in income (`refund_lines`), a negative line in its category in expenses (`checking_refund: true`, `checkingExpenseRefundLineDrafts`). Category lives on the refund (`assignFlowExpenseLineCategory`). Marked/unmarked only by the user (`POST`/`DELETE /api/income/movements/:id/refund`, «Reembolso de gasto»; `markCheckingExpenseRefund` accepts only single-leg credits on checking-list accounts).
- **A card credit is spending coming back** (`markCardRefunds`, `flowsExpenses.ts`; `isGastosRefundLine`): every negative card line except a card payment, ABONO DE DIVISAS, traspaso de deuda, or a nota paired with its purchase carries `card_refund` (the line's stored `kind`; `isCcPaymentMerchant` and the other merchant rules in the contracts' `cards/lineRules.ts` classify at write, stored-line readers read the kind: `isCardPaymentLine` / `isCardDebtTransferLine` / `isDivisasAbonoPaymentLine` in `ccPaymentLines.ts`) and counts negatively in its category (a no_cuenta/internal-transfer category stays out). Paired notas net with their purchase. Debt moved between own cards is `checking_internal_transfer`: the peso leg of «TRASPASO A DEUDA NACIONAL» and a «RECAUDACION …» paying another card's facturado (repair tool `server/scripts/archive/repair-flows-overview-cash-lines.ts`).
- **A card credit can name the purchase it refunds** (migration 365 `card_refund_links`: refund flow → purchase flow, `cardRefundLinks.ts`, `cardRefundLinkWrites.ts`). The bank prints a partial refund or an overcharge's difference with no reference to its purchase, so only the user links them: the row's ⋯ «Reembolso de…» lists the candidates (`GET /api/flows/expenses/refund-candidates?unit=&account_id=&line_id=`: the same card, the credit's currency, worth at least the credit, bought on or before its day and within 120 days; the merchant's own first, then the newest) and `PUT|DELETE /api/flows/expenses/refund-links` writes or removes the link; a credit still «sin categoría» takes the purchase's category as its «Único» (unlinking leaves it). The pipeline step «card refund links» (after «line money») turns a link into the line's `refund_of` (`manual: true`, so «Reembolso · <purchase>» and the ⓘ card); a purchase a cuota plan took over (its one-shot line superseded, `oneShotLinesSupersededByPlans`) reads as the plan's whole price, and a link no line stands for, or whose purchase is on another card (a re-homed statement), marks the credit `refund_link_broken` (the row offers «Quitar»), never failing the pipeline; an unlinked credit with exactly one candidate under its own merchant (stems holding each other, ≥ 5 letters) carries it as `refund_suggestion`, shown for one confirm with the ⋯ badge. Nothing links on its own. The credit keeps its own day (the Expenses page's rule). A link holds both its flows (migration 366 reinstalls the flow delete triggers with it; `flowHoldsAssignments` in card-line adoption counts it), so a re-import that rewrites either line folds the old flow into the new line and the link survives; every fold of a flow into another moves both ends first (`moveCardRefundLinks` in `flowIdentity.ts`: `mergeFlowInto` and `ensureFlowForKey`'s keyless-flow fold), or the link would cascade away. A purchase broken into pieces is one candidate at its whole amount; candidates are indexed by card once per pipeline run. `can_link_refund` (line shapes) gates the control; a pairing the bank's data decided (paired nota, annulment, plan prepayment) is not the user's to undo.
- **Additional cardholder charges are `additional_card`** («Tarjeta adicional», auto-tagged on origin in `additional_card_last4s`). The Santander feed's `TipoBen` → listing `holder` (absent on pending rows); an `additional` row stores the account's active additional card as origin (`activeAdditionalCardLast4ForAccount`: latest statement's additional origin; none throws), tagged the night it lists. His wires back are refunds in that category, so it nets to what he owes. «Tarjetas adicionales» summary (`additional_cards`: month/year, running balance) from lines: `isAdditionalCardChargeLine` (cc, additional origin, still `additional_card`; recategorized = user's own), `isAdditionalCardReimbursementLine`. New wires: `npx tsx scripts/propose-card-reimbursements.ts --payer-note-token=<token> [--apply]` (from `server/`).

## Card-to-card facturado payments

**A card-to-card facturado payment is one transfer** (migration 311 `card_to_card_payments`, `cardToCardPayments.ts`, `scripts/sync-card-to-card-payments.ts`): each part of a facturado payment record — a one-shot charge (line pesos and day) or a `finances_facturado` plan (principal on purchase day) — is a `pago_tarjeta` transfer paying master → paid master, recorded in `card_to_card_payments` (movement, financing link, part kind + ref; deleting the row deletes the movement). Derived, and one flow per payment: a part whose reference changed while its transfer did not (a re-import replaced the plan or the charge's flow under a new id; one stored transfer and one new part of the same record, kind, ends, pesos and day) keeps its movement and only the row's `part_ref` moves, and a transfer whose pesos, day or ends changed is written again with the old flow folded into it (`mergeFlowInto`), so it keeps its arrival in «Últimos movimientos» and what was filed on it. `syncCardToCardPaymentTransfers` creates/replaces/deletes them after every card write (after the facturado matcher), after a hand-made financing link changes, and at boot; a dollar part throws (a dollar card→card transfer is not a payment `foreignDebtPaymentsForAccount` knows). Both card lines stay evidence; every money reader skips the transfer (deposits, cash, owed, P/L unchanged). Guards: the nightly mirror converter never offers a PAGO a card-to-card transfer explains (`cardToCardPaymentEvidenceClaim`: a part, a day's parts or the whole record within `MATCH_WINDOWS.financing_payment`); the manual card-payment task refuses a card source.

## Review fixes of 2026-11-26

- **Stopped billing has one cycle of grace for every issuer** (`cardStoppedBilling`, `cards/lifecycle.ts`): the last month on file (statement or open bucket) plus one. Right after a close an idle card has no bucket for the new cycle yet, and its just-closed facturación is the one being paid.
- **Nothing is stamped for today without an anchor to walk from** (`ccCreditCardValuations.ts`): a card whose first month-end is still ahead reads today's value live (`latestCreditCardDisplayedBalance` → the billing total); no anchor and no valuation row are written from it.
- **A category spread never crosses plans** (`installmentPurchaseSiblingStatementLines`, `ccExpenseCategories.ts`): a matched line's category follows its plan's ledger lines; a line with no ledger link (a 00/N row) takes the look-alike lines only while they belong to plans of one total, otherwise only the line is filed and the manual reconcile reports `category_spread_ambiguous`.
- **Legacy pre-amount category keys:** `scripts/rekey-cc-manual-plan-legacy-categories.ts` (report-first, `--apply`, `--live`) moves a manual plan's category from the no-total `installment-h:` key to the current one; the lookup fallback in `ccManualInstallmentStatementReconcile.ts` goes once it has run on the live database.
- **Generic unique merchants** are a `dbStateMemo` (`expenseGenericUniqueMerchants.ts`); there is no invalidate call.
