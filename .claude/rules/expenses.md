## Real-estate expenses (`/flows/expenses/real_estate`)

- **Model:** `expense_accounts` = tracked places, **fully data-driven** (no slug lists): `active_from`/`active_to` (YYYY-MM), `property_account_id` → property master (owned places), `comunidad_merchant_patterns` (comma list). Personal data, live DB only. «Añadir lugar…» (POST `…/places`); picking a property attaches mortgage rows. `places[]` drives sections, series, labels. Bills = `expense_entries` with `expense_account_id`: kind in `category` (`RealEstateBillKind`), consumption in **`kwh` / `m3`** columns. `real_estate_expense_links` ties a bill 1:1 to its paying CC/checking purchase (auto by merchant patterns + amount within bill +0/+2 months, or manual).
- **Purchase-first assign** (`POST /api/flows/expenses/real-estate/assign`): the bill is created FROM an unlinked purchase plus a manual link in one tx (never invented amounts). Pool `GET …/unlinked-purchases`: default category `bills`, place's occupancy (+2-month tail), kind-pattern matches first, multi-select. `PATCH …/entries/:id/consumption`; `PATCH …/entries/:id/bill-month` (rewrites month-end `spent_on`; refuses months putting the purchase outside +0..+2); «mes anterior» toggle (bill = purchase month − 1); `DELETE …/entries/:id` (cascades). Bulk: `npx tsx scripts/suggest-real-estate-links.ts` (report-first `--apply`; contribuciones, same-day/amount dups and odd rents review-only; never MUTUARIA/METLIFE/TOKU mortgage cash; cuota splits dedupe by purchase_key).
- **Mortgage rows are read-only ledger derivations:** kind `mortgage`, `expense_entry_id: null`, amount = what the regular cuota paid (`loan_payments`), `mortgage_parts` = scheduled amortización, extra amortization (`amortizacion_ext_clp`), carrying (interés + seguros); pie/prepagos are capital, out (user's choice); the movement's CLP `amount` is an equity mark, not cash. Chart kinds `mortgage_amortization`, `mortgage_extra_amortization` (drawn below zero — saving, not cost), `mortgage_carrying`; a point's `total` = sum of absolutes. Never stored. `bill_month` = scheduled month from cuota numbers (`mortgageCuotaBillMonths`: modal anchor of month(occurred_on) − cuotaN; throws on inconsistency); `spent_on` = real payment date («pagado» hint).
- **Billed-period framing lives here only;** `/flows/expenses` uses real dates.
- **No double counting:** `flowsManualExpenses.ts` excludes place rows structurally (`expense_account_id IS NULL`).
- **A bill exists only when a whole purchase backs it** (assign, or a bill mail paired with its charge; «Total» lines, never «Por cuota» pieces or financing splits). Repairs: `server/scripts/repair-real-estate-bills.ts --plan=<json> [--apply]` (delete/remonth/assign/cover). Electricity and HOA bills sit one month before their charge (consumption month; buildings issue next month and the receipt names the issue month). **Multi-month payments are one bill with `months_covered`** (migration 245): months end at the bill month (`covers_from`), chart spreads whole pesos (`spreadOverCoveredMonths`, last month takes remainder), table «cubre …»; set via `PATCH …/entries/:id/months-covered` or the `cover` step. Metrogas bills two months. Contribuciones stay in the month paid. A refunded payment is not a bill (keep one; mark the refund in «bills»). **Broken links are reported, never deleted on read:** `findInvalidRealEstateExpenseLinks` sets `broken_link` (`missing_purchase` | `outside_window`), page offers Desvincular; only the reconcile script deletes. Bill links ride the flow on re-import (`flows/cardLineAdoption.ts`, with rejections; never onto a sibling copy) and on cartola replacement (`adoptPartialIntoCartolaRow`, `docs/untangle-flow-identity.md`).
- **Chart by kind, per scope** (`chart_scopes`, `flowsRealEstateExpenses.ts`): blocks `all` + each place; `series` `<place>__<kind>` stacked by place (date order) then `REAL_ESTATE_CHART_KINDS` (mortgage carrying, amortization, rent, gastos comunes, electricity, water, internet, gas, contribuciones, extra amortization on top); legend order = the scope's `kinds` (client keeps no copy); unknown kind throws; monthly points first→last bill. Client counts Rango back from the scope's last point, one hue per kind alternating with a desaturated copy per place (`scheme`); a place page totals that place only. Shared component `StackedExpensesChart` (bar/line, year averages, legend hides series — never changes the Total).
- **DFL-2 places and rent credits** (migration 300, `rentalPlaces300.ts`, `f22/rentals.ts`): `expense_accounts.dfl2` + `dfl2_built_m2` (≤ 140) + `dfl2_reception_year`, set together («Añadir lugar…» or `PATCH …/places/:slug/dfl2`); halving window from `legalParameters.ts` `dfl2ContribucionesHalvingYears` (20y ≤ 70 m², 15 ≤ 100, 10 ≤ 140), served as `dfl2_contribuciones_halved_through`, never stored. Income kind `rent` (`CHECKING_INCOME_KINDS`, `FLOW_KINDS`) requires its place: `upsertCheckingIncomeMovementOverride(id, {income_kind: "rent", rent_place_slug})` (`flow_income_overrides.rent_place_id`, refused otherwise); `GET /api/income/rent-places`. F22: non-DFL-2 rent → **1849** (→ **955** → 158), contribuciones → credit **1637** capped at 1849 × round(157/170, 3) (`f22PropertyTaxCreditCap`; 304 = 157 − 136 − 1637 − 162 − 1018 − 610, `f22/identities.ts`); DFL-2 rent shown, declares nothing (Suplemento 9.3 g); a third DFL-2 rented place in a year throws. Reajuste to December via `ipcReajuste`. **152 is never negative:** net negative DJ 1890 interest goes to 169 (`f22Code158` throws on negative 152). The flows overview counts rent in its own `rent` income bucket.
- **The agency's commission is a bill row** of kind `management_fee` (`RealEstateBillKind`, `REAL_ESTATE_LINKABLE_KINDS`, `REAL_ESTATE_CHART_KINDS` after `contribuciones`, label `expenses.kinds.management_fee`), created from the checking debit (`assignPurchasesToRealEstateExpenses`); `taxFacts/rentals.ts` carries it in `bills` (Schedule E line 11). Demo: one per forwarded rent (`simulate.ts` `placeBills`).

Each list fetches when shown (modals while open, big groups once expanded) and never holds another selector's rows while loading; optimistic category, big-group and note edits patch every cached list (`flowsExpensesLinesAll`). The old whole-history payload (`GET /api/flows/expenses/credit-card`, ~12 MB, refetched on every live dollar tick) is gone, and no expense route carries `/credit-card` any more (`/gastos`, `/big-groups`, `/purchase-notes`, `/purchase-big-group`, `/financing-links`, PATCH `/lines/:lineId/category`). The client's `countsTowardGastosMes` only moves lines between month-modal sections during optimistic edits, pinned by `server/src/test/gastosMesCountingCases.json` (both suites).

## Manual expenses, line pieces and big groups (migrations 235–236, `expenseLinePieces.ts`)

- **Big groups belong to the expense:** `cc_expense_purchase_big_groups` keyed by `purchase_key` alone (keys are globally unique: `line-pr:`, `installment-h:<account>:…`, `checking-mv:`, `checking-cartola:<account>:…`, `manual:<id>`, `<line key>#split:<seq>`); payroll deductions take none. Categories («Único») and notes stay keyed by (account, key).
- **Manual expenses** (`expense_entries` without a place) for spending no bank/card shows: «Gasto manual» / «Editar» in the month modal; `POST /api/flows/expenses/manual`, `PATCH`/`DELETE …/manual/:id`; note keeps `manual:` prefix (line shows the rest); deleting removes its big group. **Any core currency** (migration 316, `expenseAmountCurrency316.ts`): `amount` + `currency` (→ `currencies`), rounded via `roundToCurrency`, picker from `/api/currencies`; the line carries `amount` {value, currency}, pesos/dollars at the day's rate (`convertFlow`, source `day`; peso expense keeps buy-rate dollars); edits restate flow and evidence. Place bills stay pesos by CHECK; bill readers use `placeBillPesosSql` and throw otherwise (`placeBillAmount.ts`). Legacy `income_entries` has `amount` + `currency` (read as day flows); income `amount_usd` is a credit's own dollars, null for pesos (dollars at the day's buy rate, `incomeLineUsdLeg`).
- **Line pieces** split a card/checking purchase line into lines with own day (`cc_expense_line_splits.spent_on`, null = line's day), amount, category; big group and note on the piece key. Partial coverage allowed (`pieces_remainder` stays on the line), exceeding the line throws (`expandLineSplitsInDrafts`). Purchase lines only (never a deposit-paired portion); no piece before its line. `GET`/`PUT /api/flows/expenses/line-pieces`; `seq` is the piece's key for life; removing drops its big group and note. Single-purpose withdrawals need no pieces. **Pieces are in their line's currency** (`flow_parts.amount` in `flows.currency`; pesos by share, remainder by what's left; DTO `currency` + `line_amount`); a mismatched piece throws; loan payment parts stay pesos.

## Lider boletas → grocery catalog

- **The Boleta Digital mail is the daily Lider source** (other card charges come by statement or paste). `fetch:lider-boletas` (IMAP `contacto@info.lider.cl`, subject «boleta») stages `Boleta.pdf` + `meta.json` under `cfraser/lider-boletas/staged/<date>-<msgid>/` (ledger by message id; `--all` backfill). `parse-grocery-receipts.py` walks both staging roots; `grocery_receipt_parse.py` picks the chain from the header RUT (`RUT: 76.134.946-5` store, bare `76134941-4` hiper/delivery, `81.201.000-K` Jumbo) or, on a cropped photo, the issuer name opening a line («CENCOSUD RETAIL S.A.»; RUT and name disagreeing throws), then `grocery_receipt_lider.py` / `grocery_receipt_jumbo.py`; unknown throws. Items: barcode, description, qty (decimal; `x 1.744 KG` lines = kg items, derived per-kg price), unit price; discounts in **two scopes**: directly under an item attaches to it; after a blank line (Mi Club `RF CANJE PESOS MCL` always, whole-receipt coupons) is receipt-level (`grocery_receipts.receipt_discount_clp`), never an item's price. **Fails unless Σ items − item discounts − receipt discounts = Σ payment legs** (TARJETA LIDER BCI / EFECTIVO net of VUELTO / PESOS MICLUB CANJEADOS / TBK) **and «TOTAL NUMERO DE ARTIC VEND» = parsed count** (units by qty, each weighed item once). Date = printed local datetime, never the mail's UTC date.
- **Paper photos go through Apple Vision OCR** (`grocery_receipt_ocr.swift`, compiled on demand by `grocery_receipt_text.py` into gitignored `ingest/python/.ocr_bin/`, keyed by sha256; reads HEIC, EXIF orientation; not Tesseract — no HEIC, eng only). `assemble_lines` rebuilds rows from blocks: de-skew by median angle, rows from confident blocks, low-confidence blocks attached to the nearest row (never bridging two), digit-less low-confidence junk dropped. Per dir: `ocr.json` (blocks, cached by engine version + sha), `ocr.txt`, and **`ocr.corrected.txt`** (human repair; parsed when present, `text_source` = `ocr_corrected`); a failed re-parse deletes the stale `parsed.json`. OCR tolerances (optional `$`, `,`/`.` thousands, digit-less item-section lines skipped as `ignored_lines`) are backed by the fail-fast checks.
- **Storage** (migration 172): `grocery_receipts` + `grocery_receipt_items`. **Identity `receipt_key` = `<chain>|<receipt_number>|<YYYY-MM-DD>`** (migration 178, NOT NULL UNIQUE; formula in `groceryReceiptKey` and the 178 hook — keep in step); `source` / `source_key` belong to the owning document. Import (`storeReceiptApply.ts`, fed by `import:grocery-receipts`) ranks documents: `email`/`pdf` > `photo`; higher rank replaces the owner, lower/equal is `skipped_duplicate` (first writer wins, so photos can't ping-pong), and an identity resolving to another document's row THROWS. Roots (`defaultStagingRoots`): `cfraser/lider-boletas/staged/` and `cfraser/grocery-receipts/staged/<date>-<source>-<key>/` (meta `{source, source_key}`, parsed `chain`; missing/unknown throws). **Drop zone `cfraser/grocery-receipts/inbox/`:** `ingestGroceryReceiptInbox` (`ingest/src/grocery/inbox.ts`) moves photos (`photo`) and PDFs (`pdf`) into `staged/<mtime-date>-<source>-<sha12>/receipt.<ext>`; exact duplicates → `grocery-receipts/duplicates/`; any other file type fails the batch before anything moves. Catalog `grocery_products` + `grocery_product_aliases` (alias = (chain, barcode) else (chain, description)); **items stamp `product_id`** (`product_source` alias|manual) so alias re-points never rewrite history; assignment fills only NULL; a changed printed description returns the item to unclassified. Run by `import:cfraser-inbox` when the inbox has a file or `hasPendingGroceryReceipts`; idempotent upsert by (receipt, position). **Import stamps** `imported.json` (stamp version + parsed sha + row + statuses): current stamp with its row still present/owned → `unchanged`, no writes. Outcomes a later run can change (`pending_branch`) aren't stamped. `-- --full` ignores stamps, `-- --reparse` forces parsing, bump `IMPORT_STAMP_VERSION` to force all.
- **Card movements are per-chain** (`CHAIN_CARD_RULES`; none = `chain_items_only` unless a match rule exists). Lider: only when paid with the Lider/BCI card and dated inside the open facturación (`lastPdfBillingMonthForAccount` period end); closed months are statement-covered. Written via web paste (`cc_lider_boleta`) with the bank's exact merchant from **`boleta_sucursal_merchants`** (`cfraser/cc-cards.json`), so dedupe collides with the feed/statement. **Each printed branch rendering is its own key** (paper omits the mail's local suffix); unknown branches are never guessed.
- **Jumbo receipts are photos only and never write a card line** (Santander's feed carries the purchase; `grocery_receipt_jumbo.py`, `CHAIN_MATCH_RULES`). Store/comuna under the CENCOSUD line (head office «KENNEDY 9001» isn't the store); quantity line (`N X $price` / `0,326 KG X $price`) printed ABOVE the item (may wait one item; each item binds the first fitting waiting line, kg ±1 peso); discounts below. CREDITO pesos with a same-pesos JUMBO/CENCOSUD card line on the printed day (`findReceiptCardLine`): `matched` | `no_card_line` (> 45 days, final; else `awaiting_card_line`, retried) | `ambiguous_card_line`. **Cropped photos** (`resolveReceiptFacts`): no number → key `photo-<sha12>`; no date → the card line's date within 7 days up to the photo date (only from a `YYYY:MM:DD.<ext>` filename, `photoTakenOnFromName`, meta `photo_taken_on`), else the photo date; else declare `#! purchase_date: YYYY-MM-DD` in the correction file (never overrides a printed date; disagreement throws). `purchase_date_source` = printed | declared | card_line | photo.
- **New stores are learned from card lines, never declared first** (migration 184, `groceryBranchLearning.ts`). An unknown-branch card-paid open-month receipt is flagged, not failed: `card_line_status = 'pending_branch'` (+ `card_line_account_id`), no line, step exits 0, badge «Sucursal nueva · sin línea de tarjeta». `learnGroceryBranchesFromCardLines` runs inside `mergeCcAccountFromParsedRows` (paste, feed, statements; result `grocery_branch_learning`) and at each receipt import (a receipt pending at its month's close gets one try against the statement): exactly one candidate (one-shot, not an importer-written `<chain>-boleta|` line, not already learned from) stores (chain, branch) → merchant minus « (T)» and marks `matched`; zero stays pending; two same-day-same-amount lines or receipts → `ambiguous`, pending. Other statuses `created` / `covered`.
- **UI** `/flows/expenses/groceries` (node beside Inmuebles, shown when receipts exist): cleaning workbench (unclassified groups by occurrence, multi-select assign/create, similarity suggestions), per-product **price history** (effective / list / per-base-unit; branch, city, brand filters), receipts with items and a **source badge** (`GroceryReceiptRow.source` → `groceries.receipts.source.*`, desktop column + mobile card row). ## Checking / cuenta vista ledger anchor

- A checking balance is **derived from movements**; a synthetic **anchor** movement (`import:cartola|anchor|<period>|saldo final`, dated before the first real movement) carries the opening balance so the walk lands on the latest cartola's `saldo_final_clp`. `ensureCheckingLedgerAnchor` re-derives it when an import advances the latest cartola period.
- **The derivation MUST use the balance reader's accounting** — `sumClpThroughDate` → `signedClpDeltaForAccountMovement` (matches `account_id OR from_account_id OR to_account_id`, signs each leg, applies `flow_kind`). Anything else gives an anchor that disagrees with the display.
- **Bank posting day (migration 199, `movementBankPostings.ts`).** `occurred_on` = best evidence of when money moved; `movement_bank_postings(movement_id, account_id, posted_on, occurred_on_basis)` keeps the day a bank document filed it on one account when different, and what `occurred_on` rests on (migration 329: `evidence` = a document stated the day; `fetch` = the listing's fetch day, an upper bound a transfer mail may move earlier) (Santander's 14:00 cutoff → next workday, possibly next month). One row per (movement, account); sparse. **Readers that reconcile against a bank document read `COALESCE(posted_on, occurred_on)` via `BANK_POSTING_JOIN_SQL` / `BANK_POSTED_ON_SQL`**: anchor derivation and month table (`nonAnchorClpFlowTotals`), `checkingMovementBalanceAtMonthEnd` (`sumClpThroughBankPostedDate`), timeline months, xlsx/cartola dedupe (`partialMovementSupersededByCartola`, `findMatchingCartolaMovementNoteInDb`, the cartola import's count) and `findMatchingInternalTransferLegId` (a leg with a posting matches only that day, else the business-day window, across months). Everything else reads `occurred_on`. **Writers** record any bank date they learn (`recordBankPosting`, throws if the movement doesn't touch the account): `claimTransferLegForBankRow`, a hand transfer superseding a bank row, receipt re-dates, Fintual promotions, both mirror conversions (each deleted leg's date, its stored posting carried through conversion and undo), the checking listing (fetch day) and transfer-notice pairing (mail day). So mail-sourced writers never wait for the bank row across a month boundary. Regression: «bank posting day» in `checkingCartolaBalances.test.ts`.
- **Forward-posted rows count from today; history keeps bank dates.** The daily checking listing no longer stores a row after its fetch day (see `bank_account.movements`), but a row any other source dates after Chile today still reaches the ledger. `displayLedgerCutoffYmd` (`movementTransfer.ts`): a read before today is strict (`≤ date`), a read at/after today has no upper bound, so a value never steps twice. Applied at two choke points: the ledger balance readers (`checkingMovementBalanceClpAt`, `clpCashBalanceClpAt`, `foreignCashBalanceAt`) and the merged deposit timeline (`buildMergedDepositMap` re-dates such events to today, bank date kept as `posted_on`), so flow-adjusted P/L reads 0 for the row every day. Don't add "skip events dated > today" caps in deposit readers: they'd create the opposite phantom. **Reconciliation stays strict:** `nonAnchorClpFlowTotals`, the anchor derivation and `checkingMovementBalanceAtMonthEnd`; the deposit-match loaders in `checkingCartolaLoaders.ts` read `loadMergedDepositInflowEventsBankDated` (pairing is about real dates); the balance cache key carries the applied cutoff. Listings keep the bank date with `AccountMovementApiRow.forward_posted` («ya en el saldo»). Equity unit walks are not covered.
- **The bank's own charges are `cash_fee` (`checkingBankCharges.ts`).** Plan maintenance fee («COM.MANTENCION PLAN»), línea de crédito interest and overdraft tax are stored as `cash_fee` by both checking importers, decided from the description: deposit loaders skip the kind, so they are the account's P/L, not withdrawals. Gastos still list them. Backfill: `server/scripts/tag-checking-bank-charges.ts`.
- **Transfer rows have `account_id` NULL** (the account is in `from_account_id`/`to_account_id`), so `WHERE account_id = ?` misses every transfer leg (and the CLP leg is unsigned). Since the anchor re-derives only when a cartola advances the period, such a bug stays latent. Regression: «cartola-derived anchor with transfer legs» in `checkingCartolaBalances.test.ts`. **When touching any balance/anchor helper, check that transfer rows are in scope.**

- **One source for a checking account's movements (`listCheckingMovements` in `checkingCartolaLoaders.ts`).** Expenses, income, deposit matching, payslip pairing and income overrides ask for an account's inflows/outflows, never a document: cartola rows plus daily «últimos movimientos» rows not yet replaced (superseded-but-unpruned left out), nothing else — no anchor, no hand adjustment. When the cartola supersedes a daily row, `prunePartialMovementsSupersededByCartola` moves the payslip link and income override onto the official row (`carryMovementKeyedIncomeState`; both reference movements without a cascade, so a leftover fails the import's FK). Source document is still read from the note there and in import-side key mechanics; a structured source column would retire that.
- **Foreign checking accounts' rows are gastos and income lines too (`listForeignCheckingAccountIds` / `listForeignCheckingMovements`).** Any cash ledger in a non-peso currency (`foreignCashCurrencyOf`) under Checking accounts; rows carry `amount_native`, `currency` and pesos/dollars at the day's stored mid via `rates.ts` (`foreignCheckingAmountIn`). Dollar lines keep `amount_usd` native; third-currency lines state `amount` (`foreignCheckingLineMoney`), income credits their `native` leg (`incomeLineLegs` → `FlowLegs.native_amount`), refunds their `native`. Below, «dollar» means «foreign» for these lists; the deposits reconciliation reads dollar deposits against the dollar accounts' months only. A `usd`-kind checking account's debits are expense lines (`flowsCheckingGastos.ts`: category rules and «Único», no deposit matching, pesos at the day's `fx_daily` — a row moving no dollars or a day with no rate throws), its credits income lines (`flowsCheckingInflows.ts`), so a US pay stub pairs with its net credit; the funnel reads both lists (`loadCheckingGastosLinesForExpenses`). A single-leg dollar row with `units_delta` on a non-dollar-cash account (401(k)/HSA payroll buy) is a deposit event at the day's rate (`accountDeposits.ts`), pre-tax via `PAYROLL_FUNDED_KINDS` (`afp, afc, us_401k, hsa`). A listed single-leg `withdrawal` debit is a «used» dollar lot (`usdCashTaxLotEvents.ts`; realized under both oficios, deferred under `none`). Notes may sit on its gastos lines (`ccExpensePurchaseNotes.ts`); a credit marked a refund (`checkingExpenseRefunds.ts`) is a dollar-expense refund (`amount_usd`, pesos at the day's rate, in `refund_lines` and a negative dollar line in its category); `flowsDepositsReconciliation.ts` counts a dollar units deposit at its dollars as `accountDeposits.ts` prices it, reads «checking present» from the dollar checking months, and treats those accounts as the funding source — out of `loadNetWorthCapitalOutflowCandidates` / `loadDepositMatchCandidates`, like peso checking. Mirror pairs pair same-currency rows.

## Historical mirror pairs + movement search

- **Mirror pairs** (`/panel/mirror-pairs`): two pre-transfer-model single-leg rows (retiro + same-amount depósito on different accounts, inflow 0–5 days after outflow) become one transfer dated the **outflow day**; original legs live in **`movement_mirror_merges`** keyed by the transfer (ON DELETE CASCADE; the note is a human summary); undo is the server function `undoMirrorConversion` (no HTTP route). **Any currency:** both legs one currency, equal to its coin — pesos with no flow_kind (sign = direction), others a `withdrawal` + `deposit` single-leg pair, no units, each on a cash ledger held in that currency (positive amount, direction in `flow_kind`); an `fx_purchase` credit never pairs, nor do two currencies. The merge row's `out_amount_clp` / `in_amount_clp` hold the legs signed in the transfer's currency (legacy column names); undo writes `withdrawal` / `deposit`. Leg DTO: `amount` (signed) + `currency`; link-established pairs stay pesos. Candidates: `listMirrorPairCandidates` (`server/src/movementMirrorPairs.ts`) — high confidence = unique both directions + cartola business-day window (`bankDateMatchesTransferDate`) + no month straddle; straddles are ambiguous but never blocked (each deleted leg's date stays as the transfer's posting day on its account). Excluded legs: flow_kind/USD legs, both-legs-cuotas, dap either side, afp/afc **inflows**, deposits already in `expense_deposit_links`/`checking_gap_deposit_mirrors`/`payroll_work_earnings`, Buda/ahorro-split notes. Rejections in `movement_mirror_pair_rejections` (migration 155; cascade on full rebuilds). **Link-established pairs**: 1:1 `expense_deposit_links` (auto/manual, `checking-cartola:` keys via `movementForCheckingPurchaseKey`, equal amounts) are `linked: true` high-confidence, no window; the link cascades at conversion. Multi-allocation/partial/synthetic links and Buda-note deposits stay out. One transfer carries one `units_delta` (abs; cuota readers use `transferLegUnitsThroughDate`). **Month-precision legs** (cuenta_ahorro_vivienda, dated month-end): window = whole month ± 7 days (`monthPrecisionPairAllowed`), bank window waived, the transfer takes the **real-day (checking) leg's date** (re-import dedupe and anchors untouched; checking-straddle block skipped). `loadTransferLegSignedFlowEvents` (`accountDeposits.ts`) buckets each side on the day **that side's valuation evidence moves**: month-precision legs keep their original merge date (no −X/+X monthly couplet); **every other leg uses the transfer's own date**, since balances and `transferLegUnitsThroughDate` key off the movement (the settlement date gives a phantom ±X couplet). Month-precision kinds: `monthPrecisionAccountKinds.ts` (`accountIsMonthPrecisionDated`, import-free for `accountBucket.ts` / `flowsCheckingGastos.ts`). Converted cartola credits leave the income pool; merge transfers into checking self-resolve in the reconciliation. **Same-day siblings are indistinguishable by amount and date** (greedy lower-id pick can absorb the wrong row): ambiguous candidates carry `out_alternatives` / `in_alternatives`, shown with notes, any convertible instead (`resolveMirrorPairRef`); an **auto** link with a same-amount competitor in window is ambiguous (manual stays high); a credit classified as income (`checking_income_movement_overrides` kind / force-include) or a debit categorized as spending (a `cc_expense_unique_purchases` category outside unclassified / deposits / internal transfer / no_cuenta, or a `cc_expense_line_splits` split) is never a leg. Repair: `server/scripts/repair-mirror-merge-legs.ts --plan=<json> [--apply]` (report-first, IMMEDIATE, idempotent; `swap` a leg for its sibling, `undo` a non-transfer — both restore originals under ORIGINAL ids; undo records a rejection — or `reject`; month-end balances and anchors verified unchanged in-transaction).
- **Checking↔CC payment mirrors (`ccPaymentMirrors.ts`):** a checking «Traspaso a T. Crédito»/PAGO TARJETA debit on any account under Checking accounts (`accountIdsUnderCheckingAccounts`) pairs with the card's payment evidence — a payment line (`isCcPaymentMerchant`: exactly PAGO / MONTO CANCELADO / ABONO, the one shared rule) or the `monto_pagado_anterior` header (date via `requireHeaderPagoIso`, throws unless ISO) — exact amount, ±4 days. UI: /panel/mirror-pairs «Pagos de tarjeta», POST `/api/movement-mirrors/convert-cc-payments`. Conversion writes one transfer checking → CC master dated the **card's credit date**, `flow_kind = 'pago_tarjeta'`; the merge row keeps the cartola leg (`out_*`) and references the evidence (`in_statement_line_id` / `in_statement_id`; migration 167: exactly one in-ref). Statement evidence is untouched (the transfer's card leg is inert; CC valuations ignore movements) and `pago_tarjeta` emits **no deposit/aportes events**. Undo restores only the checking leg. **Nightly auto-convert:** `npm run convert:cc-payment-mirrors` (in `import:cfraser-inbox` after the receipt step); fail-closed; the debit's date stays as the posting day on checking. **Foreign-debt payments:** a «Egreso por Compra de Divisas» debit pairs with the card's ABONO DE DIVISAS in the bought currency (by the LINE's currency, so the web-paste bucket qualifies), by date window + one-to-one uniqueness + implied-rate band (0,5×–2× that currency's mid on the abono's day, `DIVISAS_IMPLIED_RATE_BAND`), evidence keyed in that currency (`ccPaymentEvidenceKey`: smallest coin, `amount_foreign`), traspaso-linked abonos excluded; converted to the migration-169 cross-currency shape (CLP from-leg = pesos that left checking, counter leg = the abono, dated at the card's credit date). **Khipu:** «Transf a khipu …» debits are candidates, pairing only with CLP card evidence of the exact pesos one-to-one; unpaired stay plain debits.
- **A closed month's month-end anchor adds what a later facturación bills (`unbilledAtMonthEndAdjustmentsClp` in `ccBillingBalances.ts`).** Beyond the statement frame (facturado + cupo), it adds every charge and payment dated ≤ calendar month-end that a LATER facturación bills (or none yet), chosen by billing (`facturacionMonthByStatementDate`), not a date window after the close (which misses Santander's close-day purchases — its next cycle starts ON the close day — and lines posted a cycle late). The stream includes dated header-payment legs (`cc_header_payment_legs`). The daily walk keeps its window from its anchor (`postCloseLiveBalanceAdjustmentsClp`). Re-stamp: `scripts/restamp-cc-anchor-frame.ts`.
- **A statement billed what it printed, credit included (`statementHeaderFacturado`, `normalizedPostCloseLines`).** Import, JSON writer and every billing reader (`facturadoFromStatement`, slot totals, `usdToClpAsDebt`) take the printed «Monto total facturado a pagar» whatever its sign; 0 = nothing billed; only a missing header falls back to lines (`scripts/backfill-cc-printed-facturado.ts`). A line dated ≥ 2 cycles before its statement (a nota carrying its purchase's date) takes that statement's close as its post-close date, so walk and anchors agree.
- **Dollar header payments (migration 218).** A header leg has currency + `amount` (old international statements print the abono only in the header, «ABONO REALIZADO»). `scripts/link-cc-usd-header-abono-legs.ts` (report-first) writes header abono − the statement's negative lines (refunds count, traspasos don't) as a dollar leg, dated on the period's one plausible-rate «Compra de Divisas» debit (which the converter pairs), else the previous pay-by. The walk values dollar legs at their statement's debt fx; cuota retirement reads peso legs only.
- **A dollar payment is valued at the debt fx of the facturación it paid (`normalizedPostCloseLines`):** the debt fx of the latest dollar statement closed before it (as `facturacionPaidOn` pairs), not its own statement's fx; a linked traspaso keeps its CLP twin's pesos. Missing/unreadable dollar statements: `scripts/estimate-cc-missing-usd-statements.ts --plan=cfraser/cc-usd-estimate-plan-0161.json` writes `import:estimate|…` statements with one «COMPRAS INTERNACIONALES (ESTIMADO)» charge (gap between printed balances either side) and an «ABONO DE DIVISAS» on the paying divisas debit (dollars = its pesos at the day's dólar × 1,021, the median divisas spread); textless scans get header abono from printed totals, an estimated charge and dated dollar legs.
- **Statements from outside the PDF corpus are named `import:<kind>|…`** (`isCcStatementPdfSource`): web pastes, Santander statement JSON, mail-rebuilt (`import:santander-mail|`), CMR mailed PDFs (`import:cmr|`). Import-sync coverage requires an on-disk PDF only for the rest.
- **Undated header payments get dated legs (migration 213, `cc_header_payment_legs`).** The parser dates «monto pagado período anterior» only when one body line carries it; undated headers are invisible to the cuota retirement (dated payments only). A leg: card, statement close (not row id — re-imports replace rows), day + pesos of the paying bank debit (`source = bank_debit`), or the previous pay-by (`pay_by`). `listCcPaymentEvidenceRows` and `listClpCcPaymentEventsForAccount` read legs as dated payments; pairing claims by payment key, so legs pair separately. Writer: `server/scripts/archive/link-cc-header-payment-legs.ts` (report-first, IMMEDIATE): dated payment lines in the period counted once (card groups repeat them), remainder matched exactly by unlinked card-payment checking debits in (previous close, close + 4 days], a remainder explained by a credit note skipped (BCI counts notas in «monto pagado»). A remainder ≠ debit may be a «disponible para pago» payment whose overpayment carries as negative saldo anterior. «Traspaso … a Línea Crédito» is not a card payment (overdraft line; `CC_PAYMENT_DESC_RE` still keeps it internal in checking gastos).
- **A converted payment finds its evidence by the payment, never by row id (`ccPaymentMirrorEvidence.ts`).** Re-imports give lines new ids (`cc_statement_lines.id` has no AUTOINCREMENT, ids get reused), PDFs take over JSON closes, pasted PAGOs settle into headers. Identity = card, currency, date, amount (pesos to unit, dollars to cent); the pairing side is the transfer's card leg (`to_account_id`, `occurred_on`, the USD counter leg or the pesos). `listCcPaymentMirrorCandidates` excludes converted payments by that key and throws when one has no evidence; `usdPaymentLineClpResolver` finds a line's divisas purchase via the evidence rows. `mergeCcAccountFromParsedRows`, the `--wipe` reload (one transaction) and web-paste line delete throw, naming the pairing, when they'd remove a paired payment. `in_statement_line_id` / `in_statement_id` are provenance only; `server/scripts/repair-cc-payment-mirror-evidence-refs.ts [--allow-kind-change] [--apply]` re-points stale ones and checks every pairing has evidence.
- **Movement search lives in the flows tables** (`/search` redirects home). `FlowsTable` filters: year/type/account/bucket (`bucket` = deepest portfolio-tree bucket; liability accounts resolve via kind to `liabilities_*` nodes; options carry `label`/`label_i18n_key`, rendered via `resolveNavTreeLabel`), `q` (note + account + counterpart + flow-type label), `date_from`/`date_to` (inclusive), `amount_exact` XOR `amount_min`/`amount_max` + `amount_currency` (clp default; compares the rounded |leg| in that currency, a row without that leg never matches). Consumers: the **net-worth dashboard (master view, `/api/groups/net_worth/flows`)**, group and account pages. ## What an expense row shows: names, money, payment names, category rules

**Names an expense row shows** (`expenseDisplayNames.ts`, `vendorCatalog.ts`, `expenseNameText.ts`, `chilePlaces.ts`, migrations 248–250). Display only — `merchant` stays the bank's text (rules and the receipt/real-estate matchers read it). `display_merchant`: bank clutter dropped (« (T)», «COMPRAS P.A.T.», «, SANTIAGO», a leading account number, «Agustinas », «COMPRA» / «Compra Nacional»), processor prefix moved to `merchant_processor` (MERPAGO\*, PAYU \*, HAULMER\*, DL \*, DP \*, PAYPAL \*, SQ \*…), then the vendor catalog. If the catalog doesn't know the name and the shop's or processor's own receipt is linked (Shopify, Flow, Pago Fácil, shop order mails — never a marketplace: Uber Eats, MercadoLibre, App Store), the receipt's payee becomes the merchant and the redundant label is hidden. A payslip line shows the employer without legal suffix, words spelled («Impuesto Único · Empresa Chile»). A merchant cut by the listing (15 chars on the web table and pending feed rows, spaces collapsed first, so length proves nothing) reads as the longer merchant it starts on the same account, only when all such read the same through the catalog. A checking transfer with a paired mail reads «Transf a <label>». `link_label` (`arrow`, `text`, `nights`, `inline`): a receipt's payee (never the processor; « - X» and brand + branch as «Brand (X)»; MercadoLibre sellers title-cased with «+N»; a URL's host; e-mails/placeholders nothing; a stay's city + nights), an App Store app before its tagline, a transfer by RUT through the catalog else the RUT's known name (a user-named contact over the bank's capitals, then the most frequent), companies without legal suffix, people as first name + first surname; `inline` false when the merchant already contains it. The label follows the note, the extras repeat it, the ⓘ card has full detail plus bank text. **Vendor catalog** (`vendor_catalog`: `exact` / `prefix` / `branch` «Brand (Branch)» / `rut`, folded patterns): a branch the name already sets apart («(Isidora)», after « - ») is unwrapped; a legal-form-only branch is dropped; places are spelled («PENALOLEN» → «Peñalolén»); a name possibly cut by the statement's 22-char column completes a mid-word branch to the one place it starts («VINA DEL MA» → «Viña del Mar», store numbers kept); several possible places → `branch_choices`, red ⋯ badge, «¿Qué sucursal?», the pick stored as a `choice` row (`POST /api/flows/expenses/vendor-branch-choice`; matched after user rows, before seed rows; untouched by the loader). Generic brands are migration seeds; the user's people/RUTs/places come from untracked `cfraser/vendor-catalog.json` via `server/scripts/load-vendor-catalog.ts [--apply]` (report-first, replaces all user rows; matched before seeds). `server/scripts/report-expense-link-labels.ts` lists labels, counterparties and printed merchants to grow it. **Catalog changes carry category rules** (migration 325, `vendorCatalogChange.ts`): a new name changes the vendor key («SB 731» → «SALCOBRAND»), so every change goes through `applyVendorCatalogChange` (migrations and `load-vendor-catalog.ts`, whose report run rolls back): a new key without a rule takes its lines' majority category; a card line the rule would change keeps its category as a per-line override; an uncategorized merchant takes the brand's; unreachable rules are deleted (listed). Additional-cardholder lines carry `additional_card`, never vote, and a general rule only they reach is deleted; a category change on such a line is always its own «Único» row (`ownCategoryOnly`). An exact entry «Brand (branch)» whose Brand is another entry shares its rule (`exactBrand`); a branch keeps a store number when the bank prints no place («Salcobrand (731)», «Cruz Verde (Las Condes 1234)»); renamed chains follow `docs/vendor-catalog-rebrands.md`. **In-event charges:** an event vendor charge (`IN_EVENT_VENDORS`: Glovox, Toliv) without receipt, on a ticket receipt's event start day or the next, links as a guess to that event. «Namecheap Order Summary» is a dollar receipt (`namecheap`) labelled by what it bought. Mail-written transfer notes omit the payee's bank («Transf a <payee>»); transfer notices are repaired of UTF-8-as-Latin-1 on import (`textRepair.ts`); `server/scripts/archive/sanitize-transfer-mail-notes.ts [--apply]` rewrites stored ones (copying merchant rules to the new key).

**An expense line's money is its own currency** (`lineMoney.ts`, `wireExpenseLine.ts`, contract `Money` / `LineConverted`). Wire: `amount: {value, currency}`, `converted: {value, currency, rate_source} | null` in the requested unit (every lines endpoint takes `?unit=`; null when already in it), `value_clp` (pesos, for peso documents: facturación/month modal totals, financing candidates). `rate_source`: `native`; `paid` / `live` / `pay_by` (card dollars at the facturación's paid rate, today's while unpaid, pay-by − 1 when none on file: `facturacionUsdRateWithSourceResolver`; a dollar payment line at the pesos paid, `usdPaymentLineClpResolver`); `day`. Into a third currency a line goes through its pesos and keeps their source. `amount_clp` / `amount_usd` / `amount_usd_at_expense` are server construction data (in-pipeline matchers read them); finished-line readers use `lineValueIn(l, unit)` / `lineAmount(l)` / `linePesos(l)`. The client never converts: `formatLineAmount` / `MoneyAmount` show native + converted («US$13,30 (≈ $13.083)», «≈» = `live`), only converted below 450 px of list width (container query). A peso document (utility bill, demo place bills) matches only peso lines; real-estate bills and declared recurring payments still read a dollar line's pesos. Manual expenses may be any core currency (migration 316; flow triggers read the row's amount and currency).

**A card payment is named by what it paid** (`expensePaymentNames.ts`, `payment_name` on lines, `display_brand` on `CC_ISSUERS`), from payment records, never bank text: a one-shot charge paying another card's facturado (`cc_facturado_payment_charges`) and every line of a `finances_facturado` plan («Total» and cuotas) → «Pago <brand> ·<last4>» (`pays_card`); the paid card's payment line → «Pago con tarjeta <brand> ·<last4>» (`paid_by_card`) when it is the whole facturado, or, paid in parts, one payment per charge/plan summing to it, dated within `MATCH_WINDOWS.financing_payment` of the latest paying day and matched by `ccPaymentEvidenceKey` (so a statement PAGO replacing a planted one keeps the name); the card leg of a `pago_tarjeta` from a bank account → «Pago desde <account>» (`paid_from_account`; a divisas abono by its dollars). Ambiguity names nothing. The client words it via i18n (`expenses.creditCard.paymentName.*`, `expensePaymentName`) in place of the merchant; the ⓘ card keeps store and bank text.

**Expense category rules are global, keyed on the vendor** (migration 252, `expenseCategoryRules.ts`, `bankMerchantName.ts`): `expense_category_rules` holds a general rule per vendor and specific rules per vendor + link. The vendor (`vendorRuleKey`) is the catalog brand of the bank text (a `branch` entry without its branch — every Jumbo is «JUMBO»; processor prefix off — «PAYU \*UBER EATS» = «UBER EATS»), else the cleaned name; a rule learned on a cut name reaches the full one (`vendorRuleKeyCandidates`). The link is a receipt's store (by brand), an App Store app, or a transfer recipient (RUT, else name); recipient rules ignore the transfer wording (vendor `TRANSFERENCIA`). Precedence: the line's own category («Único», per-line override) > specific rule > general rule > «sin categoría»; the general rule applies in `resolveCcExpenseCategory`, the specific one late, once the link is known (`withSpecificCategoryRules`, after the subscription step, for card and checking lines whose category only the bank name decided, including an «Único» row without category). Lines carry `category_link` (kind, key, label, `specific_default`) and `category_specific`; the picker's «Específico» checkbox (default on, off for food delivery: Uber Eats, Rappi) picks which rule a change writes, sent as `rule_link`. `server/scripts/report-expense-category-rule-migration.ts --db=<file>` lists migration 252's merge of the old per-account rules and its conflicts (read-only).
